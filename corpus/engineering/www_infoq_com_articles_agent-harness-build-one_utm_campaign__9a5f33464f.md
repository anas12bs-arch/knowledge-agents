Article: The Agent Harness: What It Is and Two Ways to Build One

This article explains the development and operational layers of an agent harness through two implementations of a finance assistant: AWS AgentCore Harness and LangChain with Envoy AI Gateway. It compares how each handles tools, memory, model access, cost control, and observability, and examines the trade-offs in operational ownership, portability, and engineering effort.   By Trista Pan
