# [infoq] Bun Rewrites 535K Lines of Zig into Rust in Four Months, Eliminates Numerous Memory Leaks

> Source: engineering | Category: engineering | 2026-09-20T21:37:18Z

Bun Rewrites 535K Lines of Zig into Rust in Four Months, Eliminates Numerous Memory Leaks

Bun creator Jarred Sumner recently announced that Bun, the JavaScript/TypeScript runtime, bundler and package manager has been rewritten from Zig to Rust. The rewrite seeks to eliminate recurrent memory safety vulnerabilities via Rust’s borrow checker. The AI-assisted rewrite was released after 4 months of work instead of the estimated one year.   By Bruno Couriol
