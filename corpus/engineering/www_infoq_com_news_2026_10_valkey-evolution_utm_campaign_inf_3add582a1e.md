> Source: engineering | Category: engineering | 2026-10-09T18:50:27Z

Olson and Söderqvist Discuss Valkey’s Evolution and Future Past Caching Use Cases at OSS EU

Valkey is a growing project, launched as a fork of Redis over two years ago, supported by twelve maintainers and nine committee members from various companies. Recent developments include enhanced throughput, improved cluster functionality, and plans for features like synchronous replication and tiered storage. The project also emphasises security and release efficiency through automation.   By Olimpiu Pop
