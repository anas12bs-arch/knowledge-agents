# [infoq] Datadog Builds a Specialist AI Model for SRE Investigations

> Source: engineering | Category: engineering | 2026-10-29T15:34:49Z

Datadog Builds a Specialist AI Model for SRE Investigations

Datadog has released Scout, a model trained to investigate production problems. It runs inside Bits Investigation, the company's AI SRE product, where it can pick up an alert, look through telemetry and suggest what caused the problem. Datadog engineers and a small number of customers are using it in a closed preview.   By Matt Saunders
