# [infoq] Presentation: Move Fast, Don’t Break Trust: Shipping Constantly with Humans and Beyond

> Source: engineering | Category: engineering | 2026-10-23T16:17:30Z

Presentation: Move Fast, Don’t Break Trust: Shipping Constantly with Humans and Beyond

Suhail Patel discusses how Monzo structures its software engineering organization and technical stack to ship safely hundreds of times a day. He shares insights into opinionated microservice abstractions, tenancy-based staging, static analysis checks, continuous profiling, demonstrating how a strong internal platform enables engineers and LLMs to iterate rapidly without sacrificing reliability.
