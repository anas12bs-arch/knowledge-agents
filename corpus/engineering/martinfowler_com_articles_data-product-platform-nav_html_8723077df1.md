# [martin-fowler] The Data Product Platform Navigator

> Source: engineering | Category: engineering | 2026-11-05T17:44:30Z

The Data Product Platform Navigator

Many organizations adopting data mesh and data products face a common
     challenge. They may be tasked with building a self-service platform for
     data products and data mesh from scratch, or they may have an existing
     platform that suffers from low adoption, preventing their data mesh from
     scaling. This article provides a platform navigator, based on the CNCF's
     Platform Engineering Maturity Model, to help you reason about your current
     situation and determine the next steps for your platform.
