Presentation: AI-Assisted Software Delivery: From Code to Production

The panelists explore how engineering teams adapt delivery architectures, pipelines, security, and governance for AI-assisted development. They share practical strategies for verifying AI-generated code, solving testing and deployment bottlenecks, maintaining human oversight, and measuring AI’s true impact on software velocity, quality, reliability, and operational cost.   By Garima Bajpaj, Martin Reynolds, Patrick Debois, Gunnar Grosch, Renato Losio
