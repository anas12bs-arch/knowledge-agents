# [infoq] AWS Introduces Enhanced Custom Event Buses for EventBridge

> Source: engineering | Category: engineering | 2026-10-12T00:46:49Z

AWS Introduces Enhanced Custom Event Buses for EventBridge

AWS recently announced enhanced custom event buses for Amazon EventBridge, allowing organizations to share a centralized bus across accounts with ordered delivery, simplified subscriber management, event replay, content-based deduplication, and a new publisher/subscriber-based pricing model.
