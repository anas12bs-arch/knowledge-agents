> Source: engineering | Category: engineering | 2026-11-05T17:44:30Z

Building a Culture for a Kubernetes-Based Platform in a Bank

In banking, open source can earn trust by running real workloads, as Marcy Paramonova and Stéphane Cusin explained in their talk Building Cloud Native Culture in a Bank at KubeCon & CloudNativeCon Europe. Deploying Kubernetes was the easy part; the hard part was culture: community of practice, shared knowledge, rituals, ownership. Teams try things, keep what works, and improve bottom-up.
