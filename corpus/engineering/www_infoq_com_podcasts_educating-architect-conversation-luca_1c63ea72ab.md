> Source: engineering | Category: engineering | 2026-11-02T20:02:36Z

Podcast: Educating an Architect: A Conversation with Luca Mezzalira

In this podcast, Michael Stiefel spoke to Luca Mezzalira about software architecture as a socio-technical discipline that balances technological design with an organization's social and managerial context. They explored how architectural choices must be adapted to a company's unique culture and team structure rather than relying blindly on universal best practices.   By Luca Mezzalira
