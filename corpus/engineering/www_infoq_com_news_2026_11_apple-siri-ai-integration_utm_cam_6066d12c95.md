# [infoq] Siri AI Developer Preview Lets iOS, iPadOS, and macOS Apps Integrate with Siri

> Source: engineering | Category: engineering | 2026-11-13T21:31:40Z

Siri AI Developer Preview Lets iOS, iPadOS, and macOS Apps Integrate with Siri

Apple released the Siri AI Developer Preview in the 27.3 beta releases of its operating systems, enabling developers and apps to integrate with the assistant and hand off actions to it. During the Preview, access to Siri AI is restricted to developer builds and internal TestFlight testers.   By Sergio De Simone
