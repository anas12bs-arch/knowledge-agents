# [infoq] Netflix at QConSF 2026: Retrofitting the SDLC for AI Coding Agents

> Source: engineering | Category: engineering | 2026-11-23T19:34:25Z

Netflix at QConSF 2026: Retrofitting the SDLC for AI Coding Agents

At QCon San Francisco 2026, Mark Khuzam of Netflix explained how the Commerce team retrofitted its SDLC so AI coding agents and engineers could ship together. The team wrote 45 architecture decision records for people and agents, added a review agent that posts only verified blockers, and serves per-domain GraphRAG context through a single MCP tool.
