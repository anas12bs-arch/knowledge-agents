> Source: engineering | Category: engineering | 2026-10-02T12:33:17Z

Article: From Reusable to Regeneratable: Rethinking the Shared UI Component Library

Regeneration has changed reuse economics. A model pointed at a solid design system can produce most standard UI on demand, so paying to keep a canonical component package alive is much harder to justify than it used to be. Keep the design system, tokens, guidelines and tests central. They are what actually buy you consistency.   By Daniel Curtis
