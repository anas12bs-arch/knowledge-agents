# [infoq] PHP Patches Two Critical RCE Flaws That Were Already Under Attack

> Source: engineering | Category: engineering | 2026-10-25T23:13:53Z

PHP Patches Two Critical RCE Flaws That Were Already Under Attack

PHP released 8.5.12, 8.4.27, 8.3.36, and 8.2.35 to fix two critical vulnerabilities allowing unauthenticated remote code execution. Amazon recorded an exploitation attempt four days before the patches shipped, and operators spent the day working out whether they ran PHP, where to get the package, and how to tell it had landed.
