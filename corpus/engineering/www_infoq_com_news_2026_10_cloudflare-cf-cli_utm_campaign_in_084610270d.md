# [infoq] Cloudflare Introduces CLI for AI Agents, Sunsetting Wrangler

> Source: engineering | Category: engineering | 2026-10-06T08:00:46Z

Cloudflare Introduces CLI for AI Agents, Sunsetting Wrangler

Cloudflare recently launched the open beta of cf, a new open-source, agent-focused command-line interface for interacting with Cloudflare services through a unified CLI. Written in TypeScript, it provides structured output and command discovery to make Cloudflare’s APIs easier for both developers and AI agents.   By Renato Losio
