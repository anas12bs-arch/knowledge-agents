# [infoq] Chainguard Launches Secure Workspaces and Checks for AI Coding Agents

> Source: engineering | Category: engineering | 2026-10-16T11:40:33Z

Chainguard Launches Secure Workspaces and Checks for AI Coding Agents

Software supply chain security company Chainguard has announced two beta products for running and testing code written by developers and AI agents. Chainguard Workspaces provides remote development environments based on hardened microVMs, while Chainguard Checks moves continuous integration into the development loop. The company announced both products at its Assemble London event.   By Matt Saunders
