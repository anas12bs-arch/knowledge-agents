# [infoq] Cloudflare Makes Basin Serverless Analytics Generally Available

> Source: engineering | Category: engineering | 2026-11-11T02:09:52Z

Cloudflare Makes Basin Serverless Analytics Generally Available

Cloudflare has announced the general availability of Basin, a serverless analytics platform built on Apache Iceberg and R2 Object Storage. The managed service supports open data formats and integrates with tools such as DuckDB, Snowflake, and Apache Spark, with no data egress fees.   By Renato Losio
