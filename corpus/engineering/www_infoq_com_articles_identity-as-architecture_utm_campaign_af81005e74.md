Article: Identity as an Architectural Concern: Enterprise IAM Beyond Authentication

The way you design your identities will drive your data models, your service scope, and your revocation policies; enterprise identity management is thus an architectural problem, not an afterthought. Based on our experience in service account governance, this article touches upon graph vs. RBAC, integrating a vault you don't own, and bounding revocation latency for an event stream.
