# [infoq] .NET MAUI 11 Makes CoreCLR the Default Runtime and Adds Incremental XAML Hot Reload

> Source: engineering | Category: engineering | 2026-11-26T08:09:20Z

.NET MAUI 11 Makes CoreCLR the Default Runtime and Adds Incremental XAML Hot Reload

Microsoft released .NET 11 with a .NET MAUI update focused on product quality. CoreCLR is now the default runtime on all platforms, and XAML gets incremental hot reload, implicit namespaces and faster resource dictionaries. The release also adds device test templates and passkey support, and raises the minimum Android and Apple platform versions.   By Edin Kapić
