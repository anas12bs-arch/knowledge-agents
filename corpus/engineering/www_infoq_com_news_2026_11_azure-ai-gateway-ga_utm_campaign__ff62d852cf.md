# [infoq] Azure AI Gateway Reaches General Availability as a Standalone Service

> Source: engineering | Category: engineering | 2026-11-28T10:14:42Z

Azure AI Gateway Reaches General Availability as a Standalone Service

Microsoft has made Azure AI Gateway generally available as a standalone Azure service for governing access to AI models and MCP tools across providers. A free tier covers 10,000 requests per gateway per month; paid billing starts January 1, 2027; and private networking remains in preview.   By Steef-Jan Wiggers
