# [martin-fowler] Bliki: LLM Voice

> Source: engineering | Category: engineering | 2026-10-29T15:34:48Z

Bliki: LLM Voice

Sometimes when I read a post, I get the growing feeling that I'm reading
  the output of an LLM. I can't necessarily define why, although there are the common
  tells (it's not the em-dashes, it's the load-bearing tapestries). As I
  do this my desire to read on atrophies, sometimes to the point of revulsion 

 There are tools out there that cliam to be able to tell the difference
  between LLM-written prose and the old-fashioned biological product. I'm wary
  of them, partly due skeptism about their accuracy, but also because it rather
  misses the point. The reaction to LLM-Voice is a human one, that sense that we
  are in an  uncanny
  valley  of prose. By the time I'd reach for a tool to tell me what I'm
  reading, it's already too late, even if it confidently tells me there's no LLM
  present. 

 I'm also suspicious of my own ability to accurately recognize LLM voice. Language
  changes over time, so a common sign of age is complaining about the language
  of the youngs. I'm inclined to bristle at prose that talks about “adopting a
  new sales  motion ,” or “a  sharp  round-up of news”. But I've heard
  these constructs coming out of human mouths, so that's more a generational
  difference rather than between carbon and silicon. (And as a transplanted Brit,
  don't get me started on Americanisms, I'm sure I'll get used to them momentarily.) 

 Right now I urge authors to be extra-wary of LLM-Voice. In particular I
  strongly recommend that authors should not fall into the trap of getting the
  LLM to write a draft. Even if it's edited by a human, it's very hard to expel
  the LLM-Voice - thus causing the uncanny valley reaction.
