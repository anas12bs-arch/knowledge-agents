# [infoq] Uber Separates Scaling Intent From Execution on Kubernetes Platform

> Source: engineering | Category: engineering | 2026-09-28T12:03:35Z

Uber Separates Scaling Intent From Execution on Kubernetes Platform

Uber has published a detailed account of its new ServiceScale controller, which allows multiple orchestrators to safely manage the scaling of the same Kubernetes workloads. The blog post, written by senior software engineers Egor Grishechko and Srikar Paruchuru, describes how the company separated scaling intent from execution to support regional failover without carrying reserved idle capacity.   By Matt Saunders
