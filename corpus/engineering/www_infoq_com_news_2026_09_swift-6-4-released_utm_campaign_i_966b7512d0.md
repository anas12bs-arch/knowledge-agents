# [infoq] Swift 6.4 Brings Subprocess 1.0, Improved Interoperability, Faster Wasm, and More

> Source: engineering | Category: engineering | 2026-09-27T15:28:40Z

Swift 6.4 Brings Subprocess 1.0, Improved Interoperability, Faster Wasm, and More

The latest release of Swift, Swift 6.4, introduces a range of language and tooling improvements, including better performance through expanded support for non-copyable values, up to 40 times faster Wasm generated code, improved interoperability with C++20 and Java, and a new Subprocess library that provides a cross-platform API for launching and interacting with external processes.   By Sergio De Simone
