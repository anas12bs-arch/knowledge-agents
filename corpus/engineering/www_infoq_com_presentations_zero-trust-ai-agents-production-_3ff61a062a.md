> Source: engineering | Category: engineering | 2026-11-05T17:44:30Z

Presentation: Zero Trust Agent Systems that Pass Audits and Still Ship

Advait Patel shares a playbook for securing AI agent systems in production. He discusses moving defense boundaries from the prompt to the tool layer, enforcing scoped workload identities, and mitigating prompt injection via tool outputs. He explains how to structure audit-ready observability and implement kill switches to protect critical data while keeping deployment fast and compliant.   By Advait Patel
