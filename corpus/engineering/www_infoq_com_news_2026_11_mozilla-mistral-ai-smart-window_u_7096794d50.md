# [infoq] Mozilla Partners with Mistral to Bring Open-Weight AI Models to Firefox Smart Window

> Source: engineering | Category: engineering | 2026-11-27T10:11:53Z

Mozilla Partners with Mistral to Bring Open-Weight AI Models to Firefox Smart Window

Mozilla has partnered with Mistral AI to offer Mistral Small 4, an open-weight model, in Firefox's Smart Window AI assistant, now in beta. It is recommended for users in France, the US and Canada. Users can choose among several models, and Mistral agreed to zero data retention. Beta feedback is mixed.   By Daniel Curtis
