Presentation: Modernising Retail at Scale: Architecting a Cloud‑to‑Edge Platform for a Global Enterprise

Jayesh Bhayani discusses Tesco’s decade-long enterprise transformation from fragmented legacy systems to a unified cloud-edge platform. He explains how aligning system, talent, and process modernizations allowed Tesco to scale mission-critical retail operations to millions of customers, while sharing practical lessons on build vs. buy, cloud cost management, and preparing for agentic commerce.
