# [infoq] Buildkite Adds Scoped Secrets and New Agentic CI Tools

> Source: engineering | Category: engineering | 2026-10-19T19:29:26Z

Buildkite Adds Scoped Secrets and New Agentic CI Tools

Continuous integration provider Buildkite has introduced pipeline-scoped secrets and new Model Context Protocol tools for validating pipelines, diagnosing failed builds and repairing webhooks. The company has also added Linux arm64 support to its GitHub Actions compatibility layer and expanded OpenTelemetry data in Buildkite Agent v4.1.0.   By Matt Saunders
