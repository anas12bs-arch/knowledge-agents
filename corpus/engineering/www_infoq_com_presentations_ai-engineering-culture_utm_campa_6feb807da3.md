Presentation: Learning Out Loud (Under Near-Existential Pressure)

Laura Savino discusses the psychological friction of AI adoption for senior engineers & architects. She explains how shifting from static expertise to learning in public lowers anxiety, accelerates tooling fluency, and prevents burnout. Leaders will discover actionable strategies to support their teams, debunk tech zealotry, and foster an adaptive engineering culture that thrives during upheaval.   By Laura Savino
