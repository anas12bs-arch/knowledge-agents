> Source: engineering | Category: engineering | 2026-11-24T15:02:06Z

CNCF Research Shows Virtual Machines Remain Central as AI Workloads Evolve

New research from the Cloud Native Computing Foundation (CNCF) and Linux Foundation Research finds that VMs host an average of 53% of production applications, while emerging AI and agentic workloads are creating additional demand for virtualisation, particularly where stronger execution isolation is required.   By Craig Risi
