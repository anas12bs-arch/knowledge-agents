# [infoq] Google EnvHarness Enables Self-Evolving Agents to Learn in Dynamics Environments

> Source: engineering | Category: engineering | 2026-10-16T21:51:09Z

Google EnvHarness Enables Self-Evolving Agents to Learn in Dynamics Environments

Google researchers open sourced EnvHarness, a framework that dynamically adapts agent-training environments to an agent's weaknesses without modifying the agent, environment, or evaluation system. According to the researchers, this approach can improve both learning effectiveness and efficiency while helping agents overcome their weaknesses.   By Sergio De Simone
