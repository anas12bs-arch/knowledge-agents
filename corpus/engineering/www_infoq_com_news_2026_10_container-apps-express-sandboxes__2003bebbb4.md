# [infoq] Container Apps Express Reaches GA on a Newly Generally Available Sandbox Layer

> Source: engineering | Category: engineering | 2026-10-01T09:13:33Z

Container Apps Express Reaches GA on a Newly Generally Available Sandbox Layer

Microsoft has made Azure Container Apps Express generally available alongside Container Apps Sandboxes, the microVM compute layer it runs on. Express skips environment provisioning and scales to zero, with subsecond startup from prewarmed pools. Custom domains, zone redundancy, Key Vault references, OpenTelemetry and Dapr are not supported.   By Steef-Jan Wiggers
