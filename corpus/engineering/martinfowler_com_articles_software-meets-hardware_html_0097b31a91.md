When Software Meets Hardware

When software meets hardware, engineering principles remain largely the
    same, but the sensible defaults often change.  Kuldeep Singh 
    describes our experience modernizing a large IoT fleet of battery-powered
    sensors. Our familiar principles remained important; what changed was how we
    applied them. These practices emerged through experiments, failures, and
    operational experience&#x2014;each challenge helping us replace a reasonable
    assumption with a more sensible default.
