# [infoq] QCon San Francisco 2026: The Harness Is the New Operating System

> Source: engineering | Category: engineering | 2026-11-23T00:22:10Z

QCon San Francisco 2026: The Harness Is the New Operating System

At QCon San Francisco 2026, Mad Labs founder and CEO Manju Rajashekhar opened the conference arguing that the harness around an AI model, rather than the model itself, decides what an agent can do and how safely it does it. The keynote mapped the parts of a harness, including context, tools, state, permissions and feedback loops, onto jobs that operating systems already perform.   By Andrew Hoblitzell
