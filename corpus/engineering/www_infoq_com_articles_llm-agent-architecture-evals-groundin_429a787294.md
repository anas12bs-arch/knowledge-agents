Article: Engineering Around Non-Determinism: Evals, Grounding, and Release Gates for AI-Drafted Suspicious Reports

Moving GenAI into high-stakes production requires treating non-determinism as an engineering problem. This architectural blueprint shows engineering leaders and architects how to build trustworthy agentic workflows using knowledge graphs, dual-layer eval judges, CI/CD release gates, and regression testing against golden datasets to automate drafting while maintaining zero-hallucination compliance.
