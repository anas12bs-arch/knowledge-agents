# [sidebar-io] Stop reporting UX activity and report business outcomes

> Source: design | Category: design | 2026-07-09T08:43:21Z

Stop reporting UX activity and report business outcomes

UX teams should report business outcomes to show impact on revenue, cost, risk, speed, retention, and to secure resources.
