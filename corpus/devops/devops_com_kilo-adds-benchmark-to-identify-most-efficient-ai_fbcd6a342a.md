> Source: devops | Category: infrastructure | 2026-06-08T21:25:12Z

Kilo Adds Benchmark to Identify Most Efficient AI Models for Coding

Kilo today made available a benchmarking framework for its open source artificial intelligence (AI) agent that enables application development teams to generate code using multiple models. Company CEO Scott Breitenother said KiloBench measures the impact that frontier AI models are having on actual production workflows versus relying on a set of benchmarks, such as SWE-bench [&#8230;]
