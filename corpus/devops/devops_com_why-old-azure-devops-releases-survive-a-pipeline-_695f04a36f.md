> Source: devops | Category: infrastructure | 2026-09-25T15:20:19Z

Why Old Azure DevOps Releases Survive a Pipeline Cutover

Old Azure DevOps Classic releases can retain retired deployment tasks, Helm names, image paths and variables long after a pipeline cutover, leaving stale redeploy paths active.
