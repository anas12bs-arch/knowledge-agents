# [devops-com] Why Plan Review Stopped Working

> Source: devops | Category: infrastructure | 2026-09-24T22:22:54Z

Why Plan Review Stopped Working

The control that held your infrastructure together was plan review, meaning a person reading a diff and deciding whether to approve it. Not the policy document and not the pipeline configuration. It worked because change arrived at human speed. That condition no longer holds. An agent opens 40 pull requests before lunch. Reviewers approve them [&#8230;]
