> Source: youtube | Category: video | 2026-11-20T23:28:49Z

WebLLM 2.0: Engineering browser AI across execution, interface, and storage boundaries

WebLLM brings local AI to browsers using MLC-LLM and TVM. As browser AI advances, systems challenges increasingly lie beyond model kernels. This talk explores runtime optimization across JavaScript, WebAssembly, and WebGPU; compiler-runtime interfaces for multimodal and hybrid models; XGrammar-based structured outputs and tool use; and OPFS-backed persistence for resumable generation.
