> Source: youtube | Category: video | 2026-11-24T04:26:43Z

Your money, your machine: Private finance AI with WebGPU and WebMCP

Wilson is an open-source AI bookkeeper that runs entirely in the browser: local models leveraging WebGPU analyze your transactions, while WebMCP exposes dashboard capabilities as tools for browser agents and the Embedding API helps with semantic search. This case study covers the harness architecture, what stays private by default, and how to selectively hand agents automation power.
