Hacking Public Wi-Fi DNS to Steal Credentials

Criminals are  hacking  into public Wi-Fi devices&#8212;at hotels, conference centers, and so on&#8212;around the world and changing their DNS settings. The goal is to redirect users to fake login pages and steal their credentials.
