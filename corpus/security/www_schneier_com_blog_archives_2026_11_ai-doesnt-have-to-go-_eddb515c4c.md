# [schneier] AI Doesn’t Have to Go Rogue to Break the World

> Source: security | Category: security | 2026-11-13T15:30:56Z

AI Doesn’t Have to Go Rogue to Break the World

This essay is adapted from  a keynote speech  delivered at the DEF CON 34 conference in Las Vegas on August 7, 2026. The essay originally appeared in  CyberScoop .  
 I want to talk about AI hacking. There are three aspects: people hacking AI systems, people using AI to hack other systems, and AIs hacking systems on their own, not even maliciously. The last two matter more than the first. The third is the weirdest and potentially the most destabilizing. 
 I started writing about this in 2021 and in my 2022 book,  A Hacker&#8217;s Mind . Back then I said it was mostly still science fiction, but not stupid science fiction. We&#8217;re seeing it more and more, especially in the past year. The trends I&#8217;m describing don&#8217;t require a major breakthrough in AI. They&#8217;re starting to happen now, and they&#8217;ll happen more often as AIs get better at autonomous problem-solving. And it will involve far more than computer code...
