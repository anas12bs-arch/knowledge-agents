# [hacker-news-sec] ToxicPanda 2.0 and GoldDigger Expand Android Banking Attacks with On-Device Fraud

> Source: security | Category: security | 2026-08-20T11:32:54Z

ToxicPanda 2.0 and GoldDigger Expand Android Banking Attacks with On-Device Fraud

Cybersecurity researchers have shed light on an updated version of ToxicPanda (aka TgToxic) that comes with "significant enhancements," including a set of 167 remote commands and expands its targeting footprint globally.

Zimperium zLabs, in a Wednesday report, said the Android malware also features a PIN harvesting workflow targeting more than 140 banking and cryptocurrency applications.
