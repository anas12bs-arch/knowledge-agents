# [schneier] The Realities of AI Video Surveillance

> Source: security | Category: security | 2026-06-30T13:18:20Z

The Realities of AI Video Surveillance

The  Financial Times  has a  good article  on how AI is changing the capabilities of video surveillance, with information from both Israel/Iran and Russia. 
 I  wrote about  this sort of thing a few years ago, how AI enables mass spying in the way that computers and networks enabled mass surveillance. The interesting development in the article is that AI allows people to ask natural language questions about video footage to AIs&#8212;and AIs can answer them. 
  In contrast with older tools restricted to a few dozen preset searches, these new tools allow an almost unlimited range of enquiries by enabling language-based searches on video...
