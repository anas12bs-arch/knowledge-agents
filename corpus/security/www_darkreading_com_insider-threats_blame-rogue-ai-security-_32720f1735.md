Is It Fair to Blame 'Rogue' AI for Security Failures?

&quot;Rogue AI&quot; terminology anthropomorphizes LLMs and shifts risk responsibility from vendors. Defenders should treat agents as untrusted, nondeterministic software systems, not sentient beings with malicious intent.
