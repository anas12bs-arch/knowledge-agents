# [hacker-news-sec] France Blames Iran for Sabotage Attack on Refrigeration Controllers at About 700 Retail Sites

> Source: security | Category: security | 2026-11-10T10:07:19Z

France Blames Iran for Sabotage Attack on Refrigeration Controllers at About 700 Retail Sites

An October cyberattack on refrigeration controllers at about 700 French retail sites was carried out by a cyber unit of Iran's Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps (IRGC), the French government said yesterday.

The attackers broke into controllers on the night of October 11 and changed their defrost settings to high temperatures. That caused food losses and, in at least 15 cases, fires.
