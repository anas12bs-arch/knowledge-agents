How the CISO-CMO Alliance Builds Trust Before Crisis Strikes

Cybersecurity and brand reputation are inextricably linked, and organizations that establish regular touchpoints, develop joint crisis communications plans, and translate security risks into their brand impact will significantly outperform those treating security as merely an operational IT concern.
