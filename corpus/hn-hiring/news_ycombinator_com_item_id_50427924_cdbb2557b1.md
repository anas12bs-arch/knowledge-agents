Customers switch to solar and a battery with nothing to pay upfront: kit, install, finance and long-term protection in one package, and the battery trades energy on the grid every day to push their bills towards zero. Founded in 2022, £113m Series A (equity and debt) in 2025, more than 3x growth last year, nearly 90 people.<p>I lead engineering and data science. Software runs every step of the customer journey: the quote, the finance application, the contract, the install and the battery trading. TypeScript, NestJS, React&#x2F;Next.js, PostgreSQL and AWS are a plus but not required. Right to work in the UK required.<p>The interview is built for how we work. No leetcode.
