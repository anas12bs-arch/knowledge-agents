# [finextra] Firms object to shares being part of OKX-ICE tokenised stock trading venture

> Source: finance | Category: finance | 2026-10-28T19:12:16Z

Firms object to shares being part of OKX-ICE tokenised stock trading venture

A host of companies, including JPMorgan Chase, Goldman Sachs and Microsoft, have registered their objection to their shares trading on a planned tokenised platform developed by Nyse parent Intercontinental Exchange and crypto exchange OKX.
