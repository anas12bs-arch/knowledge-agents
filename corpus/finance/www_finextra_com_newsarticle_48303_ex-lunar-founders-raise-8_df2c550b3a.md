# [finextra] Ex-Lunar founders raise €8.2m to launch AI-native audit firm Repodo

> Source: finance | Category: finance | 2026-08-26T09:25:04Z

Ex-Lunar founders raise €8.2m to launch AI-native audit firm Repodo

Ken Villum Klausen, Peter Andreasen and Joachim Strøjer Hansen, who co-founded and helped build Nordic digital bank Lunar as CEO, CFO and CPO respectively, have raised €8.2 million in pre-seed funding to launch Repodo, a new authorised audit firm built around AI from the ground up.
