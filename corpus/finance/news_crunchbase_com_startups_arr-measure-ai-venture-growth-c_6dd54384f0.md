# [crunchbase] What To Measure When ARR Isn’t Enough

> Source: finance | Category: finance | 2026-11-18T16:26:05Z

What To Measure When ARR Isn’t Enough

AI companies’ hybrid subscription, usage and outcome-based pricing makes headline ARR figures an unreliable measure of predictable revenue, often obscuring differences in customer commitments and spending patterns. Guest author Kenza Zayani, an early-stage investor at RTP Global, warns that as these businesses seek growth capital, investors increasingly expect evidence of retention, expansion, revenue predictability and sustainable margins to assess the durability of growth.
