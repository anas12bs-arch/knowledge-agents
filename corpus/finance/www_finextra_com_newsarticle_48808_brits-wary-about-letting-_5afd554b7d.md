# [finextra] Brits wary about letting AI agents make payments for them

> Source: finance | Category: finance | 2026-11-25T05:45:02Z

Brits wary about letting AI agents make payments for them

Brits are increasingly turning to AI to research purchases but scepticism remains when it comes to the checkout, with just 13% saying that they are likely to let an agent make a payment for them in the next year.
