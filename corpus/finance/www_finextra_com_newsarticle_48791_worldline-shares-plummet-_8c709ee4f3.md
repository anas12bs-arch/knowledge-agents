# [finextra] Worldline shares plummet on FT report on cash advances

> Source: finance | Category: finance | 2026-11-20T17:55:06Z

Worldline shares plummet on FT report on cash advances

Shares in Worldline fell by as much as 20% on Friday after the Financial Times reported that the French firm delayed payments to suppliers and asked customers for cash advances ahead of its financial results in the years leading up to a 2023 profit warning.
