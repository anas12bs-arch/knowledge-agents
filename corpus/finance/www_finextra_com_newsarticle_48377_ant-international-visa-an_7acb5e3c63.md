# [finextra] Ant International, Visa and Mastercard to develop 'Know-Your-Agent' framework

> Source: finance | Category: finance | 2026-09-10T11:45:18Z

Ant International, Visa and Mastercard to develop 'Know-Your-Agent' framework

Ant International, Mastercard, and Visa have begun collaboration on a Know-Your-Agent (KYA) interoperability framework, designed to help card networks, digital wallet ecosystems, agent platforms and marketplaces streamline agent onboarding and identification across networks.
