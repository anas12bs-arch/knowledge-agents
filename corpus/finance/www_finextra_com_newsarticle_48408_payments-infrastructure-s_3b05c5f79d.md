# [finextra] Payments infrastructure startup Fin.com emerges from stealth with $20m funding

> Source: finance | Category: finance | 2026-09-15T23:42:11Z

Payments infrastructure startup Fin.com emerges from stealth with $20m funding

Fin.com, a financial infrastructure company building an orchestration layer that lets businesses collect, convert, and move money across borders through local payment rails, USD virtual accounts, Swift and stablecoin settlement, has emerged from stealth with $20 million in funding.
