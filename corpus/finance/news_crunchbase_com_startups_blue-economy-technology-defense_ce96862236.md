# [crunchbase] The Blue Economy: The World’s Quietest Economic Engine And Its Smartest Cities

> Source: finance | Category: finance | 2026-11-19T12:28:32Z

The Blue Economy: The World’s Quietest Economic Engine And Its Smartest Cities

The $2.6 trillion blue economy is creating startup opportunities in maritime automation, clean energy and logistics, with blue tech funding nearing $10 billion through Q3 2026, driven largely by major defense deals. Alberto Onetti, chairman of Mind the Bridge, writes that ports are emerging as hubs for testing and scaling these technologies, but realizing their potential requires coordination among port authorities, startups, investors and policymakers.
