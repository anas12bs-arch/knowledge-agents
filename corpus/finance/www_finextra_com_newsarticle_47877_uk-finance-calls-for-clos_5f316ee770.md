# [finextra] UK Finance calls for closer UK-EU ties

> Source: finance | Category: finance | 2026-06-08T14:38:55Z

UK Finance calls for closer UK-EU ties

Ten years after the Brexit referendum, UK Finance, in partnership with Freshfields, has called on the EU and the UK to put financial services on the agenda for the upcoming Leaders’ Summit, and place it within the political reset to build a more strategic financial services relationship.
