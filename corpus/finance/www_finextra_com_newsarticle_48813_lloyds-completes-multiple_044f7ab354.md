# [finextra] Lloyds completes multiple digital trade collections between Bangladesh and the UK

> Source: finance | Category: finance | 2026-11-25T16:07:46Z

Lloyds completes multiple digital trade collections between Bangladesh and the UK

Lloyds has teamed up with Prime Bank and vendor Enigio to carry out multiple cross-border documentary collections for apparel shipments from Bangladesh to a UK garment wholesaler, entirely in digital form.
