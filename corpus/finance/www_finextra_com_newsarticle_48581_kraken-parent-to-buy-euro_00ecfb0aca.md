# [finextra] Kraken parent to buy European bank Saldo

> Source: finance | Category: finance | 2026-10-14T15:21:09Z

Kraken parent to buy European bank Saldo

The parent of crypto exchange Kraken has struck a deal to buy Finland-based Saldo Bank in a move that would enable the firm to offer deposits and lending products in Europe.
