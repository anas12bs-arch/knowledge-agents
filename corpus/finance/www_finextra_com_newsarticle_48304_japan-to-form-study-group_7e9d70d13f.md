# [finextra] Japan to form study group for blockchain-based settlement of stock and bonds

> Source: finance | Category: finance | 2026-08-26T11:07:54Z

Japan to form study group for blockchain-based settlement of stock and bonds

Japan's FSA, finance ministry and central bank are planning to develop a national blockchain-based settlement infrastructure for stocks and Japanese government bonds (JGBs), the Nikkei reported Wednesday.
