# [finextra] Convicted FTX founder Bankman-Fried applies for pardon

> Source: finance | Category: finance | 2026-06-08T21:25:11Z

Convicted FTX founder Bankman-Fried applies for pardon

Sam Bankman-Fried has officially asked President Donald Trump for a pardon two years after being sentenced to 25 years in prison for stealing $8 billion from customers of FTX, the bankrupt cryptocurrency exchange he founded.
