# [finextra] BoA fined $1.2bn over "egregious" AML deficiencies

> Source: finance | Category: finance | 2026-10-14T15:21:09Z

BoA fined $1.2bn over "egregious" AML deficiencies

Bank of America (BoA) has been fined $1.2bn for its "deficient and ineffective" anti-money laundering (AML) process as well as "egregious and continued" violations of the Bank Secrecy Act (BSA).
