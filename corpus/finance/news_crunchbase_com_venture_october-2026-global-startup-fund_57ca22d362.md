# [crunchbase] China’s Largest Startup Deal Ever Lifts Global Venture Funding To $59B In October

> Source: finance | Category: finance | 2026-11-04T02:24:31Z

China’s Largest Startup Deal Ever Lifts Global Venture Funding To $59B In October

Global venture funding in October totaled $59 billion, a 45% jump from a year earlier, per Crunchbase data, in large part due to a record-setting $15.9 billion funding round for China’s DeepSeek.
