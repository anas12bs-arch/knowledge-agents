# [finextra] Circle to buy Singapore-based cross-border payments platform Tazapay

> Source: finance | Category: finance | 2026-09-08T17:29:43Z

Circle to buy Singapore-based cross-border payments platform Tazapay

Stablecoin-issuer Circle has agreed to buy Tazapay, a Singapore-headquartered B2B cross-border payments infrastructure provider. Financial terms of the deal were not disclosed.
