# [finextra] Polymarket lobbies Europe to treat prediction markets as financial products, not gambling

> Source: finance | Category: finance | 2026-09-22T23:43:10Z

Polymarket lobbies Europe to treat prediction markets as financial products, not gambling

US prediction market Polymarket has been meeting regulators in the UK and Europe to make the case that it should be treated as a financial services provider rather than a gambling firm, according to the Financial Times.
