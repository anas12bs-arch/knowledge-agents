# [finextra] Socure hits $5.2bn valuation on investment; buys AI fraud platform Fravity

> Source: finance | Category: finance | 2026-08-28T00:53:08Z

Socure hits $5.2bn valuation on investment; buys AI fraud platform Fravity

Socure, the identity and risk intelligence infrastructure platform, has raised funding from investors including Goldman Sachs Alternatives and Wells Fargo at a $5.2 billion valuation. The firm has also acquired Fravity, an agentic platform that automates fraud, risk, and compliance operations.
