# [finextra] EPC to create EU Digital Identity Wallet framework for PSPs

> Source: finance | Category: finance | 2026-10-19T19:29:26Z

EPC to create EU Digital Identity Wallet framework for PSPs

The European Payments Council (EPC) has greenlit work on a standardisation framework designed to support the consistent acceptance of EU Digital Identity Wallets by payment service providers.
