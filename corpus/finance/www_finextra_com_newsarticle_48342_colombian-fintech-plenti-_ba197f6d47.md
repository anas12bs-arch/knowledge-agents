# [finextra] Colombian fintech Plenti raises $3 million in Tether-led seed round

> Source: finance | Category: finance | 2026-09-03T09:13:15Z

Colombian fintech Plenti raises $3 million in Tether-led seed round

Plenti, a Colombian fintech focused on currency movement and investment services, today announced a $3 million seed funding round led by Tether, the issuer of the USDT stablecoin, with participation from Verda Ventures.
