# [crunchbase] General Catalyst Takes The Lead Over Y Combinator In Backing $5M+ Fintech Deals

> Source: finance | Category: finance | 2026-07-24T12:05:44Z

General Catalyst Takes The Lead Over Y Combinator In Backing $5M+ Fintech Deals

For the first time in several quarters, General Catalyst in Q2 overtook Y Combinator when it came to participating in the most fintech deals of $5 million or more, per Crunchbase data. The quarter also marked the busiest one for General Catalyst since 2021 in terms of investing in rounds of $5 million or above.
