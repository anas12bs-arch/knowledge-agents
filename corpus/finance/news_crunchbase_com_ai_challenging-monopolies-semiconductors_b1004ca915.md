# [crunchbase] How To Challenge A Monopoly In The AI Age

> Source: finance | Category: finance | 2026-11-06T22:03:56Z

How To Challenge A Monopoly In The AI Age

AI chip startups can challenge the sector's Goliath's by building substantially better hardware for emerging inference demands, much as Juniper challenged Cisco with a new architecture suited to the internet’s rapid growth. To win customers, writes guest author R K Anand, co-founder and chief product officer of Tensordyne, challengers must prove their advantages, make switching easy and use small, agile teams to anticipate needs that incumbents have yet to address.
