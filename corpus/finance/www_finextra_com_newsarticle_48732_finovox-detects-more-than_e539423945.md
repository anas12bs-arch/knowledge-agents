# [finextra] Finovox detects more than €100,000 in AI-related fraud every month

> Source: finance | Category: finance | 2026-11-10T17:43:43Z

Finovox detects more than €100,000 in AI-related fraud every month

Finovox, a European specialist in AI-powered document fraud detection that helps organisations identify falsified and manipulated documents and strengthen their verification processes, has found that document fraud is now that sophisticated that AI-related fraud is at an annualised rate of over €1.2 million.
