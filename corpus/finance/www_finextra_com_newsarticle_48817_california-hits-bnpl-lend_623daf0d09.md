# [finextra] California hits BNPL lender Afterpay with $2.7m penalty

> Source: finance | Category: finance | 2026-11-26T00:13:54Z

California hits BNPL lender Afterpay with $2.7m penalty

The California Department of Financial Protection and Innovation (DFPI) has hit BNPL provider Afterpay with a $2.68 million penalty for "illegally" failing to do ability-to-repay underwriting while making loans to new customers.
