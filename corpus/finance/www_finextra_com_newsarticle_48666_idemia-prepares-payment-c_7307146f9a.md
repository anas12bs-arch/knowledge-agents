# [finextra] Idemia prepares payment cards for quantum threats with EMVCo certification

> Source: finance | Category: finance | 2026-10-29T15:34:49Z

Idemia prepares payment cards for quantum threats with EMVCo certification

Becoming the first company to achieve EMVCo certification for a payment card platform that provides access to post-quantum cryptography (PQC) and crypto-agility services, Idemia can now enable banks to future proof their cards against quantum threats.
