# [crunchbase] Socure Secures $156M at $5.2B Valuation, Acquires AI Fraud Investigation Startup Fravity

> Source: finance | Category: finance | 2026-08-28T00:53:07Z

Socure Secures $156M at $5.2B Valuation, Acquires AI Fraud Investigation Startup Fravity

Identity verification and fraud prevention company Socure announced Thursday that it raised $156 million in a strategic growth investment valuing it at $5.2 billion. The Incline Village, Nevada-based company also says it is acquiring agentic AI startup Fravity, which will be incorporated into its RiskOS platform as RiskOS_Agents.
