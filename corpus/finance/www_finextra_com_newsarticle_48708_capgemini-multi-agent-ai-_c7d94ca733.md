Capgemini: Multi-agent AI moves into banking and insurance, but orchestration remains limited

Financial institutions are struggling to scale multi-agent AI beyond pilot projects, with just 10% of banks and insurers having achieved enterprise-wide orchestration despite 59% piloting or deploying the technology, according to Capgemini research.
