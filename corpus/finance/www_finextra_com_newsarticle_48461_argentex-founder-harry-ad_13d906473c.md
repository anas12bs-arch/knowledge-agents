Argentex founder Harry Adams raises £7.5 million for new startup Tenora

Harry Adams, founder of collapsed foreign exchange firm Argentex, has secured £7.5m in funding for his new fintech venture Tenora, as the startup expands its platform beyond foreign exchange into the wider corporate treasury market.
