# [finextra] Revolut rolls out euro stablecoin

> Source: finance | Category: finance | 2026-08-26T08:40:17Z

Revolut rolls out euro stablecoin

Revolut has begun a phased rollout of its first stablecoin, euro-backed EURR, to eligible customers in Denmark, Poland, and Portugal, with wider availability in other EEA markets expected later this year.
