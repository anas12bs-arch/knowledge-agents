# [finextra] Crédit Agricole buys Worldline out of merchant payments JV

> Source: finance | Category: finance | 2026-07-05T23:16:19Z

Crédit Agricole buys Worldline out of merchant payments JV

Crédit Agricole has bought out Worldline to take 100% ownership of Cawl, the merchant payment services joint venture the two established in 2023. Financial terms were not disclosed.
