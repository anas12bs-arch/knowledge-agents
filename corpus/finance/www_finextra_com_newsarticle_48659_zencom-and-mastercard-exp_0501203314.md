# [finextra] Zen.com and Mastercard expand Pay By Bank for merchants and consumers across Europe

> Source: finance | Category: finance | 2026-10-28T13:50:35Z

Zen.com and Mastercard expand Pay By Bank for merchants and consumers across Europe

European fintech Zen.com has enabled Pay by Bank experiences through Mastercard Open Finance, now supporting merchant payment acceptance and digital wallet top ups across nine target markets, Germany, Austria, Spain, Belgium, the Netherlands, Italy, Portugal, Ireland and Lithuania.
