# [finextra] UK fintech Kord raises £6.4 million

> Source: finance | Category: finance | 2026-07-08T09:14:01Z

UK fintech Kord raises £6.4 million

Kord, a UK-based fintech focused on identity verification and payment processing for high value real estate transactions, has raised £6.4 million in Series A funding led by Guinness Ventures, with Beringea, SFC Capital, and angel investors also joining.
