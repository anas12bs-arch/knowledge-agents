Would I lie to you? ANZ warns of troubling ‘scam-coaching’ trend

ANZ is warning customers about a troubling rise in 'scam‑coaching' – a tactic where criminals train victims to bypass bank security by giving them scripts, cover stories, and instructions on how to respond to bank staff.
