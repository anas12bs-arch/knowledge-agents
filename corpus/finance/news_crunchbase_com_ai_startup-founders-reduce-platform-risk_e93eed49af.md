# [crunchbase] How Founders Can Reduce Platform Risk Before It Threatens Growth

> Source: finance | Category: finance | 2026-11-12T12:10:02Z

How Founders Can Reduce Platform Risk Before It Threatens Growth

Founders should treat dependence on app stores as infrastructure risk, since a sudden removal can disrupt customer access, subscriptions and revenue with no reliable path to reinstatement. Sharing his experience, Yakov Filippenko, founder and CEO of Intch, suggests that building and testing alternative access and payment channels before a crisis can help keep the business operating.
