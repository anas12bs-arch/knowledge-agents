# [finextra] Swiss telcos form fraud risk consortium

> Source: finance | Category: finance | 2026-07-09T08:43:19Z

Swiss telcos form fraud risk consortium

Swisscom and Sunrise, Switzerland’s two largest telecom providers, have established the world’s first fraud risk consortium with LexisNexis Risk Solutions, enabling them to exchange real-time intelligence on fraudulent events.
