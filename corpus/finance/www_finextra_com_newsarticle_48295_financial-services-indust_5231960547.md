# [finextra] Financial services industry publishes roadmap for equity issuance electronification

> Source: finance | Category: finance | 2026-08-25T08:54:36Z

Financial services industry publishes roadmap for equity issuance electronification

In a step towards the first fully electronic IPO, the FIX Trading Community (FIX) and the Investment Association (IA), have published an industry best practice framework  to support the modernisation of equity capital raising processes.
