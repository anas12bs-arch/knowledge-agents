# [finextra] Anchorage Digital expands global services with Standard Chartered

> Source: finance | Category: finance | 2026-10-06T01:33:38Z

Anchorage Digital expands global services with Standard Chartered

Digital asset custody services provider Anchorage Digital has formed a partnership with Standard Chartered through which it will offer USD accounts and access to Swift wires, including transfers to and from third parties, to eligible non-US institutional clients.
