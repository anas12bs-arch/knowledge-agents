# [finextra] Paymob garners $35 million in pre-Series C

> Source: finance | Category: finance | 2026-09-21T13:13:28Z

Paymob garners $35 million in pre-Series C

Egypt-based fintech Paymob, has finalised a $35 million pre-Series C funding round co-led by Mubadala Investment Company, an Abu Dhabi-based sovereign investor, and the European Bank for Reconstruction and Development.
