# [finextra] UK competition watchdog raises concerns over Brink's-NCR Atleos deal

> Source: finance | Category: finance | 2026-09-30T23:45:36Z

UK competition watchdog raises concerns over Brink's-NCR Atleos deal

The UK's competition watchdog says it will undertake an in-depth investigation into cash management giant Brink's' planned $6.6 billion deal to buy ATM operator NCR Atleos - unless the parties take action to address concerns.
