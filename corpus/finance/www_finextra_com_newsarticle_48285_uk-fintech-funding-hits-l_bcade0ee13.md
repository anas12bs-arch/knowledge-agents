# [finextra] UK fintech funding hits lowest level in a decade

> Source: finance | Category: finance | 2026-08-23T23:16:09Z

UK fintech funding hits lowest level in a decade

UK fintech funding dropped to its lowest level in a decade in the first half of 2026, with firms receiving just £1.8 billion in investment, according to the latest KPMG figures.
