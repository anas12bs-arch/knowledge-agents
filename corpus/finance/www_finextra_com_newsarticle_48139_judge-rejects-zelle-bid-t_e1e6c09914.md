# [finextra] Judge rejects Zelle bid to dismiss lawsuit over fraud losses

> Source: finance | Category: finance | 2026-07-23T14:56:39Z

Judge rejects Zelle bid to dismiss lawsuit over fraud losses

A US judge has rejected an effort from the operator of Zelle to dismiss a lawsuit filed by New York Attorney General Letitia James accusing the bank-backed P2P payments service of failing to protect users from "massive amounts of fraud".
