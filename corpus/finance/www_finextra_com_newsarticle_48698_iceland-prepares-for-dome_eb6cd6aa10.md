# [finextra] Iceland prepares for domestic account-to-account payments infrastructure

> Source: finance | Category: finance | 2026-11-04T17:28:39Z

Iceland prepares for domestic account-to-account payments infrastructure

The Central Bank of Iceland is preparing for new payments infrastructure that would enable banks and payment service providers (PSPs) to offer account-to-account (A2A) payments for goods and services, as the Nordic country looks to diversify its retail payment channels and strengthen financial resilience.
