# [crunchbase] North America’s Startup Funding Falls In Q3 As AI Giants Eye The Public Markets

> Source: finance | Category: finance | 2026-10-07T16:21:17Z

North America’s Startup Funding Falls In Q3 As AI Giants Eye The Public Markets

In total, investors poured $92 billion into seed- through growth-stage rounds for U.S. and Canadian startups in the third quarter, per Crunchbase data. That’s a 35% decline from the prior quarter but up 50% from year-ago levels.
