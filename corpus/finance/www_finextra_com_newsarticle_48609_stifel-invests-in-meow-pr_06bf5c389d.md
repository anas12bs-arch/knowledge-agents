# [finextra] Stifel invests in Meow; preps white label treasury platform

> Source: finance | Category: finance | 2026-10-20T11:59:55Z

Stifel invests in Meow; preps white label treasury platform

Stifel Financial has led a $31.6 million Series B finding round for business banking fintech Meow Technologies. The investment is part of a wider deal that will also see Stifel Bank offer a white-label version of Meow’s treasury management platform to its commercial banking clients.
