# [finextra] Orange becomes first merchant in France to offer Wero for bill payments

> Source: finance | Category: finance | 2026-09-02T09:57:24Z

Orange becomes first merchant in France to offer Wero for bill payments

Orange has become the first merchant in France to offer subscription bill payment with Wero, a solution developed by the bank-backed European Payments Initiative (EPI) and initially designed for payments between individuals.
