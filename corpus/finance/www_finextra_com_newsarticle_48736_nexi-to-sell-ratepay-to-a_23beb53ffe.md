# [finextra] Nexi to sell Ratepay to Affirm

> Source: finance | Category: finance | 2026-11-10T17:43:43Z

Nexi to sell Ratepay to Affirm

Italian payments giant Nexi has agreed to sell German fintech Ratepay to buy now, pay later player Affirm. Financial terms were not disclosed.
