# [finextra] Stripe and HSBC drive contactless payments for UK small businesses

> Source: finance | Category: finance | 2026-11-10T17:43:43Z

Stripe and HSBC drive contactless payments for UK small businesses

According to HSBC UK research conducted by Censuswide among 594 small business owners with turnover below £2 million who are involved in processing customer or client payments or transactions, 33% have lost a sale because they could not accept a customer's preferred payment method.
