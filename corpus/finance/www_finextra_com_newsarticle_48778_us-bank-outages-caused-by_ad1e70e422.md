> Source: finance | Category: finance | 2026-11-18T16:26:06Z

US bank outages caused by software bug at JPMorgan Chase

The service outages that hit a number of US banks earlier this week were the result of a software bug at JPMorgan Chase. Meanwhile, experts have claimed that the rash of such outages in recent days - including at Microsoft - may have been caused by firms rushing to patch issues identified by AI.
