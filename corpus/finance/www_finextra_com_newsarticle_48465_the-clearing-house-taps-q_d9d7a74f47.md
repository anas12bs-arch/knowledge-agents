# [finextra] The Clearing House taps Quant for tokenised deposit network

> Source: finance | Category: finance | 2026-09-24T15:16:53Z

The Clearing House taps Quant for tokenised deposit network

The Clearing House has picked UK-based programmable money infrastructure provider Quant to power a new interoperable payments network that will enable US financial institutions of all sizes to clear and settle tokenised deposit transactions.
