# [crunchbase] Public Markets Are Frequently Wrong. The SaaSpocalypse Shows Us Why

> Source: finance | Category: finance | 2026-11-19T12:28:32Z

Public Markets Are Frequently Wrong. The SaaSpocalypse Shows Us Why

The “SaaSpocalypse” selloff and uneven recovery exposed public markets’ difficulty distinguishing software companies that benefit from AI from those it threatens, particularly as deeply embedded platforms retain competitive advantages. In this guest essay, Richard Anton, co-founder and general partner of Oxx, argues that this volatility distorts private transaction pricing, undermines confidence in public ownership and makes IPOs less appealing to founders.
