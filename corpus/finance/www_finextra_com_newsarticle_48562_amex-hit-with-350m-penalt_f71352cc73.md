# [finextra] Amex hit with $350m penalty for AML deficiencies

> Source: finance | Category: finance | 2026-10-09T18:50:27Z

Amex hit with $350m penalty for AML deficiencies

The Office of the Comptroller of the Currency (OCC), the main banking regulator in the US, has issued a $350m civil money penalty against American Express National Bank for deficiencies in its anti-money laundering (AML) and bank secrecy act (BSA) processes.
