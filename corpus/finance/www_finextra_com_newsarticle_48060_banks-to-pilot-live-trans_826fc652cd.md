# [finextra] Banks to pilot live transactions on Swift's blockchain-based shared ledger

> Source: finance | Category: finance | 2026-07-09T08:43:19Z

Banks to pilot live transactions on Swift's blockchain-based shared ledger

Seventeen banks from six countries are preparing to pilot live transactions on Swift's interoperable blockchain-based shared ledger, using tokenised deposits for 24/7 payment availability and better liquidity efficiency.
