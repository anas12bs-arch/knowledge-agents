# [finextra] Enfuce enters Ireland with Payac migration

> Source: finance | Category: finance | 2026-06-04T10:51:45Z

Enfuce enters Ireland with Payac migration

Payac, the Irish payments and financial services provider supporting credit unions across Ireland, is migrating its 150,000 current accounts to a cloud processing platform managed by Nordic-based processing platform Enfuce.
