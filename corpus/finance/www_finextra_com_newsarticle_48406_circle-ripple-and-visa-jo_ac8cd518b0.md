# [finextra] Circle, Ripple and Visa join $10 funding round for stablecoin platform Velocity

> Source: finance | Category: finance | 2026-09-15T23:42:11Z

Circle, Ripple and Visa join $10 funding round for stablecoin platform Velocity

UK stablecoin startup Velocity has raised $10 million in an extension of its Series A round, with investment from Visa Ventures, Circle Ventures, Ripple, Haun Ventures, Translink Capital and Mirana Ventures.
