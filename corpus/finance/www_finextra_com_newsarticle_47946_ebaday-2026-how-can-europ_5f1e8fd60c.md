EBAday 2026: How can Europe achieve both innovation and sovereignty in payments?

The strategic roundtable: ‘Rethinking innovation and sovereignty in European payments’, moderated by Olivier Denecker, executive advisor, included speakers Carlo Bovero, global head of cards and retail payments, BNP Paribas; and Mark McNulty, head of UK and Europe payments and operations transformation, Citi as they discussed how Europe can balance the sovereignty agenda with the innovation needed to compete globally.
