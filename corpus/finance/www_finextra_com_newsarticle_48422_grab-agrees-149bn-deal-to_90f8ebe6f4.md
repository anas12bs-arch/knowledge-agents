# [finextra] Grab agrees $1.49bn deal to buy majority stake in Apac BNPL platform Atome

> Source: finance | Category: finance | 2026-09-17T00:39:10Z

Grab agrees $1.49bn deal to buy majority stake in Apac BNPL platform Atome

Grab, the Singapore-based firm that began life as a ride-hailing app before expanding heavily into financial services, has agreed to purchase a 60% stake in buy now, pay later provider Atome Financial for $1.49 billion in cash.
