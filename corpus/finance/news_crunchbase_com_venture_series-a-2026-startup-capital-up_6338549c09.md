Series A In 2026: Capital Is Flowing Faster, But Fewer Startups Are Making The Cut

More money is pouring into Series A rounds, but it isn’t opening the door to more startups. U.S. companies have raised $42 billion at that stage this year through October, up roughly 60% year over year, while deal counts remain on pace with the past few years, Crunchbase data shows.
