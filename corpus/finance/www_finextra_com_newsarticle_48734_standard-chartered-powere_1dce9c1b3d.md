# [finextra] Standard Chartered powered bank Mox appoints Anshul Sabherwal as CEO

> Source: finance | Category: finance | 2026-11-10T17:43:43Z

Standard Chartered powered bank Mox appoints Anshul Sabherwal as CEO

The former head of product and payment of Standard Chartered Hong Kong Anshul Sabherwal has been appointed CEO of Mox, a digital bank licensed in Hong Kong, powered by Standard Chartered in partnership with PCCW, HKT and Trip.com.
