# [finextra] EPC publishes new Verification of Payee matching recommendations

> Source: finance | Category: finance | 2026-11-04T09:31:03Z

EPC publishes new Verification of Payee matching recommendations

The European Payments Council (EPC) has published an updated set of recommendations for matching processes under its Verification Of Payee (VOP) scheme, providing additional guidance on character handling and the assessment of beneficiary names ahead of payment initiation.
