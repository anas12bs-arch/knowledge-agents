# [finextra] Zafin to buy Naehas to expand AI-powered banking and compliance platform

> Source: finance | Category: finance | 2026-10-27T22:19:28Z

Zafin to buy Naehas to expand AI-powered banking and compliance platform

Zafin, a Toronto-based AI platform for regulated institutions such as banks, has inked a deal to buy Naehas, a provider of offer management, disclosure automation and marketing compliance technology serving financial institutions in the US.
