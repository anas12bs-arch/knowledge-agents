# [crunchbase] Exclusive: Numeracle Raises $28M To Keep Legitimate Business Calls From Spam Label

> Source: finance | Category: finance | 2026-10-28T13:50:35Z

Exclusive: Numeracle Raises $28M To Keep Legitimate Business Calls From Spam Label

Numeracle, a McLean, Virginia-based company that verifies businesses and links their identities to the phone numbers they use to contact customers, has raised $28 million from Edison Partners to help businesses whose calls are incorrectly labeled as spam or blocked by phone carriers.
