# [finextra] Indonesia ranks social engineering scams as single biggest concern

> Source: finance | Category: finance | 2026-11-11T17:11:31Z

Indonesia ranks social engineering scams as single biggest concern

With the majority of scams originating on social media and messaging apps, a new Biocatch survey of 100 fraud management, AML and compliance team leaders at banks in Indonesia has found that social engineering scams remain the biggest concern.
