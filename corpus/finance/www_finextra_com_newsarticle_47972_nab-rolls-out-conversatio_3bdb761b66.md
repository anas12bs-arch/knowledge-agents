NAB rolls out conversational AI; creates AI cybercrime hub

NAB is rolling out a conversational AI tool from Databricks that enables staff to explore data by asking simple questions. At the same time, the bank is tackling the downside of the AI boom with a new hub that brings together frontline responders across technology, cyber security, fraud, payments and physical security to keep ahead of rapidly evolving AI-enabled threats
