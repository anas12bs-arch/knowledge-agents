VC Firm Plural Closes On $1.1B To Invest In European Startups With Potential For ‘GDP-Level Impact’

London-based startup investor Plural, with a portfolio mix of energy, compute, defense, robotics, health and software,
says it has closed on its largest fund to date — just over $1.1 billion, or 1 billion euros — to lead early-stage investments across Europe.
