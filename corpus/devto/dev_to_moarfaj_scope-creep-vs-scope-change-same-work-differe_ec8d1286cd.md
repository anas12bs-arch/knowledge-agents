# Scope creep vs scope change: same work, different paperwork

> Source: devto | Category: news | 2026-11-21T12:33:20Z

Scope creep is growth nobody agreed to. A scope change is the same growth, agreed in writing with price and dates. A change order is the record that turns one into the other.
