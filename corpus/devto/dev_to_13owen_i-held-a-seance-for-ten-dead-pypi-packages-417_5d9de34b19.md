# I held a seance for ten dead PyPI packages

> Source: devto | Category: news | 2026-10-27T22:19:37Z

427 packages in PyPI's top 15,000 haven't uploaded anything since before 2018. Ten of them pass their own tests on Python 3.8 and fail them on 3.13. An open-weight model raised six of them, for 83 cents all told.
