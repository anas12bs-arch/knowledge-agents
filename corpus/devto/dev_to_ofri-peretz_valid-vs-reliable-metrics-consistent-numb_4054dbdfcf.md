# Valid vs. Reliable Metrics: Consistent Numbers Can Still Be Wrong

> Source: devto | Category: news | 2026-07-20T14:27:15Z

A clock that runs ten minutes fast is perfectly reliable — the same wrong time every read — and completely invalid. Reliability and validity are independent properties, and confusing them is how confident dashboards stay green while the thing they measure quietly fails.
