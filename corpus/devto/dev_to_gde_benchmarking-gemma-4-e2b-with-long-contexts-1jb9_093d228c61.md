# Benchmarking Gemma 4 E2B with Long Contexts

> Source: devto | Category: news | 2026-11-27T01:52:26Z

Gemma 4 E2B loses most of a long document: as served on an 8 GB RTX 3050 it found 52 of 80 hidden facts at 16k tokens and 14 at 32k. Thinking on helps and still leaves it short. Keep its documents under 16k tokens, or use the 12B.
