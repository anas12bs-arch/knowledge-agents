# Bun 1.4.4's bun fmt checks Prettier's own repository about 280 times faster than Prettier

> Source: devto | Category: news | 2026-10-30T17:09:13Z

Bun 1.4.4 ships a built-in formatter. Checking Prettier's own 2,623-file source tree took 0.10-0.15s under bun fmt against 36.77s under Prettier itself, but it also silently reimplements only a short whitelist of Prettier plugins.
