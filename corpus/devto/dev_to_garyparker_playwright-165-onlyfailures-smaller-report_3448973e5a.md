> Source: devto | Category: news | 2026-11-14T19:32:25Z

160 runs of a 2,000-test suite on Playwright 1.65.1. onlyFailures cut the JSON report from 3.05 MB to 158 KB at 20 failed tests. It also cut the totals, and the durations other tools read.
