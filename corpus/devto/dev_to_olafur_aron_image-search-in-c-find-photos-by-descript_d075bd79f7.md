# Image Search in C#: Find Photos by Description with CLIP, Offline

> Source: devto | Category: news | 2026-11-04T09:31:15Z

Search a folder of photos by typing what is in them, from C#, with no cloud API, no Python and no ONNX export. CLIP runs in your process from one NuGet package. Tested on 1,000 COCO photos: 19 ms per photo to index, and the right photo in the top 5 for 85% of captions.
