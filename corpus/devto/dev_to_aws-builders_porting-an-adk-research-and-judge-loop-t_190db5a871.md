# Porting an ADK Research and Judge Loop to Strands on Bedrock AgentCore, With A2A 1.x

> Source: devto | Category: news | 2026-11-28T02:06:51Z

A three-agent researcher, judge and course builder moved from Google ADK to Strands Agents on Amazon Bedrock: Nova Web Grounding for search, a Strands graph for the loop, AgentCore Runtime for the agents, ECS Express Mode for the web app, and A2A 1.x from an unmerged Strands pull request.
