# I misspelt 12 footballers' names. Postgres full-text found 4, Meilisearch found all 12.

> Source: devto | Category: news | 2026-09-13T17:39:41Z

Loaded 1,760 real players into Postgres full-text, Postgres trigram, and Meilisearch, then typed their names badly. Full-text found 4 of 12, trigram found 8 but returned confidently wrong players, Meilisearch found all 12 and was faster.
