> Source: devto | Category: news | 2026-10-15T05:11:11Z

WAMR3D is a Rust and WebAssembly 3D engine with its own WebGPU renderer and a three.js-style API. Change one import, and batch your hot paths for up to about 8x less draw-submission CPU time than three.js WebGPU.
