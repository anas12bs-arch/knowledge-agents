# Stop Shipping Search Boxes With a Race Condition

> Source: devto | Category: news | 2026-11-07T14:25:44Z

Type fast into a search box wired to fetch(), and the result on screen isn't from your last keystroke — it's from whichever request happened to finish last. That's a race condition, and the fix is a one-line guard.
