# stela — six Lithair releases in five days, because a product demanded them

> Source: devto | Category: news | 2026-09-08T09:16:30Z

I started building a blog engine on it, and in five days the framework went from v1.3.0 to v1.9.0 — not out of ambition, out of necessity: every gap the product hit became a release. What stela is, what building a real consumer reveals that no code review finds, and where it honestly stands: it works, and it isn't shipped.
