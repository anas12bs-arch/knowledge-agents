# Stop Fixing Headline Gaps With Negative Margins. Use `text-box-trim`

> Source: devto | Category: news | 2026-10-02T12:33:26Z

Line-height reserves space for accent marks your headline never uses — a hand-tuned negative margin only fixes it for one font at one size. text-box-trim does, natively, from the font's own metrics.
