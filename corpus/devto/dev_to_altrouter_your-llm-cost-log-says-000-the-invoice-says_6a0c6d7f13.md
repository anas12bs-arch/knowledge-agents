# Your LLM cost log says $0.00. The invoice says $1,320.

> Source: devto | Category: news | 2026-11-03T09:22:32Z

A typical LLM call costs a fraction of a cent, so a money column with two digits after the point rounds it away. At list price, 1,000 tokens in and 200 out cost $0.0044 on GPT-5.6 and log as $0.00, while $0.008 on Claude Opus 5.5 logs as $0.01, 25% too much. Store tokens or integer micro-dollars per call and round once, when you add them up.
