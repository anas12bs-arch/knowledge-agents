# Same Video File, Same Threshold, Three Answers: Caption Safe-Zone Frames Are Matched by Grid, Not by Count

> Source: devto | Category: news | 2026-09-12T07:04:10Z

A caption safe-zone check failed a clean video clip, and re-running the identical check three different ways produced three different answers on the same file, because the two renders being compared were sampled on independently-computed timestamps instead of a shared frame index.
