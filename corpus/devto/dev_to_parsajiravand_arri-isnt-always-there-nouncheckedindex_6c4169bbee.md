# arr[i] Isn't Always There. noUncheckedIndexedAccess Says So.

> Source: devto | Category: news | 2026-11-27T19:30:24Z

TypeScript types arr[i] as T, never T | undefined — even though the array might be empty. The noUncheckedIndexedAccess flag makes bracket indexing tell the truth.
