# The Null Result Belonged To The Other Hypothesis

> Source: devto | Category: news | 2026-08-28T12:19:03Z

I ran an experiment to test why some animation frames were rendering empty, got "no change at all," and filed that as confirmation of my theory. It was the opposite — a null result is evidence for whichever hypothesis predicted it, and mine had predicted a change.
