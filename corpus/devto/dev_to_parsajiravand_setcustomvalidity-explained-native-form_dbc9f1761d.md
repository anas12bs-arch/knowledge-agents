# setCustomValidity() Explained: Native Form Error Messages

> Source: devto | Category: news | 2026-11-27T19:30:25Z

"Please fill out this field" looks different in every browser and can't be styled or reworded from CSS — so most teams rip out native form validation entirely. setCustomValidity() lets you keep it and still write the message yourself. There's one gotcha that makes it worse than doing nothing if you miss it.
