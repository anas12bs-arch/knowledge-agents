# Eighteen hours to close the loop

> Source: devto | Category: news | 2026-10-04T18:45:22Z

A new Postgres test container in Lithair's CI fails as if its privileged flag were ignored. The bug is in cidx, the CI runner that builds Lithair. Issue, two fixes, release 3.8.0, and Lithair consumes it eighteen hours after opening the issue. Fourth time that week the loop ran that way, while Lithair shipped seven releases in seven days.
