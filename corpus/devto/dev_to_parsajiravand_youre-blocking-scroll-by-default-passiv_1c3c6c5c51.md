# You're Blocking Scroll by Default. `passive: true` Fixes It.

> Source: devto | Category: news | 2026-11-06T22:04:06Z

Every touch and wheel listener you add can make the browser wait before it scrolls — because it has to check whether you'll call preventDefault(). `passive: true` tells it you won't, and scrolling stops waiting on your code.
