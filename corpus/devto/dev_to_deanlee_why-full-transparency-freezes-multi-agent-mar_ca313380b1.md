# Why Full Transparency Freezes Multi-Agent Markets

> Source: devto | Category: news | 2026-11-06T22:04:04Z

A Stanford and UMD study reveals that handing frontier LLMs complete information triggers aggressive rent extraction in double auctions, cutting realized market welfare by up to thirty-five percentage points.
