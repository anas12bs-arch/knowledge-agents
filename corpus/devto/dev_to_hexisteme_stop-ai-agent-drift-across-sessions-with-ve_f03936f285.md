# Stop AI Agent Drift Across Sessions With Versioned, Grep-able Rules

> Source: devto | Category: news | 2026-07-15T05:43:27Z

An AI agent gives a correct answer Monday and a different one Friday in a fresh session — that's drift, and better prompts don't fix it. Reusable Decision Units are versioned markdown files with grep-able triggers that fire a committed judgment automatically, instead of trusting the model to remember.
