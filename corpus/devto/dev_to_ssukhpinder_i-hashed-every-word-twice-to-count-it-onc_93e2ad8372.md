# I Hashed Every Word Twice to Count It Once

> Source: devto | Category: news | 2026-08-04T09:46:54Z

A word-frequency counter hashes every key twice without meaning to. CollectionsMarshal.GetValueRefOrAddDefault does it once, and on 5 million tokens that's about 1.7x faster for free.
