# Attackers Heated French Supermarket Fridges to 50 C Using a Login Bypass Based on the Date

> Source: devto | Category: news | 2026-10-15T05:11:11Z

Hundreds of French supermarkets had their refrigeration controllers hijacked overnight, spoiling food and starting fires. The flaw that let attackers in wasn't a stolen password — it was a login code computed from the device's own clock.
