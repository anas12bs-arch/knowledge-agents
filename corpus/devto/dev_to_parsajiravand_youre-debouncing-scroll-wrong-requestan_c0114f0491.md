# You're Debouncing Scroll Wrong. requestAnimationFrame Fixes It.

> Source: devto | Category: news | 2026-10-26T18:54:28Z

Debouncing a scroll handler makes it wait until scrolling stops — which is why your sticky header always lags half a beat. requestAnimationFrame fixes the lag by syncing updates to the browser's own paint clock instead of a timer.
