# We Measured the Lifespan of 7,144 Software Versions. The Median Is 18 Months.

> Source: devto | Category: news | 2026-07-18T11:20:04Z

Original research from the endoflife.ai dataset: across 463 products, the median supported lifespan of a software version is just 1.5 years. Hardware lives longest, cloud services die fastest, and Internet Explorer outlived everything.
