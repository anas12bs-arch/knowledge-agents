# Taint vs. Heuristic Detection: The Difference Between a Proof and a Hunch

> Source: devto | Category: news | 2026-07-20T21:01:18Z

A linter flags fetch(userUrl) because of the variable's name — it has never seen the data. Taint analysis hands you a proven path; a heuristic hands you a resemblance. Confusing those two confidence levels is how a CVSS 9.1 ends up printed as LOW.
