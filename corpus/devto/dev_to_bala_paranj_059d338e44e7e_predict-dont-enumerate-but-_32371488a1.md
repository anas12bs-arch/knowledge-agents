> Source: devto | Category: news | 2026-06-16T23:15:40Z

Michael Roytman's case for prediction over enumeration is correct for the questions that are genuinely uncertain. But some security questions aren't uncertain at all. They're deducible. Routing them through a prediction model throws away certainty you could have had.
