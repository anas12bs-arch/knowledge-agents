# Five resampling filters on one product photo: 5.4x on edge error, and sharpening makes it worse

> Source: devto | Category: news | 2026-11-24T23:49:52Z

A 1200 px product photo roundtripped through five PIL filters at three ratios. Edge error separates 5.4x while whole-image error stays under 1%, ringing only appears past 1/4, and sharpening narrows the edge 45% while tripling the distance from the master.
