# Still in the Game: Why We Keep Coding Against the Machine

> Source: devto | Category: news | 2026-07-04T09:37:50Z

There is a particular kind of quiet that settles in when you watch an AI model write, in seconds, code that would have taken you an hour. It is not despair exactly. It is closer to the feeling of standing next to someone who runs faster than you will ever run. The question that follows is simple and uncomfortable: what am I still doing here?
