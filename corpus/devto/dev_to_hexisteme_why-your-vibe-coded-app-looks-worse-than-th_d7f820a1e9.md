# Why Your Vibe-Coded App Looks Worse Than the Showcases: A Forensic Audit

> Source: devto | Category: news | 2026-07-20T19:56:23Z

A solo builder ran a 6-agent forensic audit over 92 of their own sessions to explain why a vibe-coded game looked worse than AI showcase demos, and found the bigger cause was silent infrastructure decay, not bad prompting: exhausted generation credits quietly swapped in code-approximated shapes, a local generation server was down with a required model file missing, and an asset pipeline was never run on one project for six-plus weeks.
