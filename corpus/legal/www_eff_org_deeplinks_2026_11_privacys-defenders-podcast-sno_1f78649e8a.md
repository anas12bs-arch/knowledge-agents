# [eff] Privacy's Defenders Podcast: Snowden Exposes NSA’s Lies

> Source: legal | Category: legal | 2026-11-17T17:57:14Z

Privacy's Defenders Podcast: Snowden Exposes NSA’s Lies

By 2013, EFF had spent seven years arguing in court that the National Security Agency was sweeping up ordinary Americans’ communications, including through    the secret room    that whistleblower Mark Klein had exposed. The government would neither confirm nor deny it, and outside the courtroom the public seemed to have moved on.      
  Then, in early June, documentary filmmaker    Laura Poitras    and journalist    Glenn Greenwald    mysteriously missed a    Freedom of the Press Foundation    board meeting at EFF’s offices. It turned out they were in Hong Kong, meeting an NSA contractor who had been secretly writing to Laura for months. His name was Edward Snowden, he had thousands of secret documents proving the government had in fact been spying on millions of Americans. When he subsequently revealed himself, a photo of him sitting behind his laptop – emblazoned with a big red EFF sticker – went worldwide.     
  Welcome to the fourth episode of “Privacy’s Defenders,” a podcast about the people – lawyers, journalists, hackers, and others – who’ve fought to secure your digital liberties since before most people even knew what the internet was.
