🗣 A Victory for Freedom of Speech Online | EFFector 38.18

No one should have to think twice before criticizing the government online. But a mass surveillance program known as "Catch and Revoke" was created to make millions do just that. EFF and other defenders of free speech are fighting back. In  our latest EFFector newsletter , we're talking about a key court win for our lawsuit  challenging this unconstitutional surveillance program .
