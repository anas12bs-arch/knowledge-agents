# [eff] Why VPNs Are Worth Defending

> Source: legal | Category: legal | 2026-10-30T21:30:06Z

Why VPNs Are Worth Defending

Developers of  virtual private network (VPN)  apps are  reporting  that their products are again being pulled from Apple’s App Store in Russia. This is an unacceptable crackdown on a vital tool to protect privacy and speech online. 
 Why? VPNs give people a way to control their own online activities. When you visit a website through a VPN, that website only sees the VPN server's location (well, IP address) rather than your actual location. It's like sending a letter through a P.O. box so the recipient doesn't know where you really live. It can also disguise where you’re accessing information from,  which can make it possible  to access information banned by your government. 
 This is an unacceptable crackdown on a vital tool to protect privacy and speech online. 
 Going after tools that give people the space to communicate and access information freely is a common tactic from the totalitarian playbook. This latest demand comes after several steps the Russian government’s taken to surveil speech specifically on Apple products. In 2024, the  Moscow Times  reported that Russia’s state media watchdog, Roskomnadzor, went after VPN apps on the Apple App Store, after banning access to Western social media platforms and blacklisting opposition media sites. Russia then  blocked Apple’s FaceTime software  in 2025, according to Apple Insider. 
  Bloomberg also recently reported  that the Russian government has added Apple and Microsoft to its state surveillance registry. This gives Roskomnadzor access to local users’ correspondence,  Apple Insider reported , and puts both companies in a position to turn over customer information to the country’s security services as required. 
 With government control encroaching on their devices and less access to tools that give them alternate methods, people in Russia are left with shrinking options to communicate online or find information that isn’t state-sanctioned.
