# [eff] Victory! You Can Now Exclude Apps from Siri AI

> Source: legal | Category: legal | 2026-10-28T23:11:36Z

Victory! You Can Now Exclude Apps from Siri AI

In the most recent update to Apple’s devices, iOS 27.2, the company has introduced a new setting to “Exclude Apps” from Siri AI, fulfilling one of our asks from    our Encrypt It Already campaign earlier this year   .  
  One of our main concerns with new AI features on devices is how those features interact with end-to-end encrypted content, particularly with secure messaging apps. Many AI features require cloud processing, and despite    privacy protections engineered into these systems   , the content of secure messaging apps may leave a user’s device without their knowledge. That changes the privacy concerns around using those tools. In a perfect world, users would be able to put a barrier up to ensure data from certain apps doesn’t leave the device. Here was our initial ask:  
   You should be able to tell the operating system’s AI to not access an app, even if that comes at the “cost” of missing out on some features. The setting should be straightforward and easy to understand in ways the Gemini and Apple Intelligence controls currently are not.   
  At launch, we found the settings to control Siri AI’s app access    behavior pretty confusing   . You had to toggle several options to block Siri AI off from accessing an app, or disable Siri AI entirely. Turning off Siri AI and    reverting to Siri Classic    is, for now, still a good option if you’d prefer to use the older version.  
  The most recent update is far more clear and does more to protect your data. And, just as expected, it comes at the cost of some features. That’s a good thing, actually. The features not working shows that the new setting is effective.   
  Here’s how to add apps to the exclusion list, once you’ve updated to 27.2:  
 
  Open    Settings    &gt;    Siri    &gt;    Excluded Apps   
  Tap “Add Apps” and select any apps you’d like to add to the exclusion list
