> Source: legal | Category: legal | 2026-10-29T20:30:39Z

Tech Tips for Parents this Digital Parenting Week

In today’s digital world, the internet is an    essential resource    for young people to access information, explore community, and find themselves. But we know that this often comes alongside fears of experiencing online harms like doxxing, grooming, or bullying.  
  Trying to protect your family from everything all the time is impractical and exhausting, but some thoughtful planning can help families devise a workable plan that meets their needs and addresses their concerns.  
  That’s why this Digital Parent Week, we’re sharing easy tips on how parents and their children can work together to co-curate a digital safety plan that helps all members of the family enjoy the benefits of technology while protecting their online privacy and safety.   
  Start with Open Conversations  
  Online safety begins with communication, not just restrictions. Talk with a young person about how they use the internet: their favourite apps, games, and social media platforms for example. Creating a supportive environment helps young people feel comfortable asking for help if something goes wrong online.  
  Together, you can think about:  
 
   What do we want to protect?    e.g., I want to protect a young person’s privacy and mental health when they’re playing Roblox. They love Roblox and use it to communicate and hang out with their best friend, so we’d like to find a way to make it as safe as possible.  
   Who do we want to protect it from?    e.g., messages from strangers, staying up too late playing, wasting money on pointless digital junk in games.  
   How bad are the consequences if we fail?    e.g, losing some money on a scam or having creepy conversations with strangers that could escalate into something more nefarious or dangerous.
