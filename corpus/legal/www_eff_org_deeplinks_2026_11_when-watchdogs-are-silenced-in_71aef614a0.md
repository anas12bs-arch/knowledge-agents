# [eff] When the Watchdogs Are Silenced: India's Crackdown on Those Documenting Online Censorship

> Source: legal | Category: legal | 2026-11-06T10:00:54Z

When the Watchdogs Are Silenced: India's Crackdown on Those Documenting Online Censorship

On November 3, our colleagues at the Software Freedom Law Center, India (SFLC.in)    received notifications    from X that the Delhi Police had issued takedown requests targeting 42 URLs. Among the accounts and posts targeted were those belonging to    SFLC.in   , the    Internet Freedom Foundation    (IFF), and X account @geoblockedindia, as well as posts documenting legal notices and instances of online censorship.     
  Takedown requests targeting SFLC.in and other digital rights groups threaten not only their work, but the public’s ability to know when and how online speech is being restricted.     
  The requests cite grounds including hate speech, alleged defamation of the Hindu nationalist organization    Rashtriya Swayamsevak Sangh    (RSS), and concerns relating to safety and security. But the context in which these requests have emerged is particularly troubling: they target organizations and individuals whose work involves documenting restrictions on online expression and making those restrictions visible to the public.     
  In other words, the organizations being targeted are among those trying to tell the public when and how its speech is being censored.     
  SFLC.in is one of India's most important digital rights organizations and promotes innovation and open access to knowledge by helping developers make great free and open-source software, protects privacy and civil liberties for Indians by educating and providing free legal advice, and helps policymakers make informed and just decisions about use of technology. In 2025, we recognized their work with an    EFF Award    for Defending Digital Freedoms.     
   IFF    (the Internet Freedom Foundation) is another of EFF's close allies in India. Like EFF, IFF combines advocacy campaigns and strategic litigation to advance digital rights. They write frequently about internet shutdowns and have filed several lawsuits contesting them.
