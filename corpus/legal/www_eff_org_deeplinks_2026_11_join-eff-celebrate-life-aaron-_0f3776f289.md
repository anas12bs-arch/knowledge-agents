# [eff] Join EFF to Celebrate the Life of Aaron Swartz

> Source: legal | Category: legal | 2026-11-06T02:54:55Z

Join EFF to Celebrate the Life of Aaron Swartz

Join us to  celebrate the legacy of Aaron Swartz  on Nov. 8, 2026. With the organizers of  Aaron Swartz Day , we’re honoring his life with an in-person and remote hackathon. This event is supported by several organizations that carry on his work, including the Internet Archive—which is hosting the in-person event—the  Freedom of the Press Foundation ,  Creative Commons  and the  Free Law Project . 
 Aaron Swartz was a  digital rights champion  who believed in the need for an open internet. His life was cut short in 2013, after federal prosecutors charged him under the  Computer Fraud and Abuse Act  (CFAA) for systematically downloading academic journal articles from the online database JSTOR. Facing the prospect of a long and unjust sentence, Aaron died by suicide at the age of 26. 
 EFF was proud to call Aaron a friend and ally. We continue to stand with programmers and developers engaged in cutting-edge exploration of technology through our  Coders' Rights Project . 
 Hackathon participants can contribute code, documentation, and design to several open-source and public-interest projects. These include  Secure Drop ,  Open Library ,  Quantifying the Commons ,  Onion Press , and our own  Atlas of Surveillance .  EFF Director of State Affairs Hayley Tsukayama will also join hackathon participants at the Internet Archive at 11 a.m. for an Atlas of Surveillance on-boarding session. 
 Contributors across experience levels are welcome. The Aaron Swartz Day organizers are also open to adding new projects and workshops, so please contact them at  aaronswartzday@gmail.com  if you’d like to suggest a workshop or project—particularly open-source projects on Github that participants can continue to work on after the event. For more details on how to participate in-person or remotely, please visit the Aaron Swartz Day and International Hackathon page at  www.aaronswartzday.org .
