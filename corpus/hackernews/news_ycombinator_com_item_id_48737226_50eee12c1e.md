Just hit this today and I&#x27;m furious. I installed the Cursor iOS app to see what it had.<p>I&#x27;ve been on `Privacy Mode (Legacy)` for a long time, which is the &quot;Do not store my code&quot; setting. A year or so ago they marked this as &quot;Legacy&quot; and hid it under a &quot;Extra options&quot; menu. They added a new Privacy Mode which is significantly more wishy-washy about what it might store - it includes a clause of &quot;Code may be stored for Background Agents or Other Features&quot;, so I&#x27;ve never touched it or wanted to enable it.<p>pic: https:&#x2F;&#x2F;us1.discourse-cdn.com&#x2F;cursor1&#x2F;optimized&#x2F;3X&#x2F;0&#x2F;9&#x2F;09412f800cb07a713d13c034f40eaf28e165e8f4_2_1032x1000.jpeg<p>Upon installing and logging in to the iOS app, my account was changed to the softer Privacy Mode and the previous setting I was on has disappeared from all menus. I contacted support and they&#x27;ve said:<p>&gt; Really sorry about this. When you set up the mobile app, the prompt to turn on Cloud Agents switched you from Privacy Mode (Legacy) to our current Privacy Mode,<p>&gt; without making clear what that meant or that it&#x27;s hard to undo. That wasn&#x27;t right, and we&#x27;re working on making that prompt clearer.<p>&gt;<p>&gt; To be straight with you: I&#x27;m not able to switch your account back to Privacy Mode (Legacy).
