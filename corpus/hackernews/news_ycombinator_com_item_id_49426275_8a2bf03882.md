# How I Read Books

> Source: hackernews | Category: news | 2026-08-24T22:37:42Z

Score: 5 | Comments: 3

I think many people misunderstand how to use books generally. They&#x27;re such simple things. Many people think you have to read every page of a book starting at the first page and go to the last page, before moving on to the next. Maybe because that&#x27;s what they were taught in school?<p>How I read:<p>- as many books as I want concurrently (from 0 to infinity)<p>- sometimes I skip entire chapters to get to the bits I find important<p>- sometimes I read parts of a book and decide it&#x27;s a bad book, or not useful to me, so I put it aside<p>- sometimes I read the same paragraphs&#x2F;pages&#x2F;chapters&#x2F;books multiple times, rarely, but occasionally, many, many times (e.g. some essays I read approximately every 3 months, since they&#x27;re so important to keep front of mind).<p>- very often, I take notes, often many pages of notes. These are an amazing resource for years to come
