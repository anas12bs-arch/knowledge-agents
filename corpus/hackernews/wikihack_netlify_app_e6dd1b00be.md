# Show HN: Wikihack – HackerTube, but for Wikipedia articles mentioned on HN

> Source: hackernews | Category: news | 2026-11-02T10:03:05Z

Score: 165 | Comments: 46

After seeing Karpathy&#x27;s Hackertube and Hackerbooks, I just had to make Wikihack.<p>I scraped all mentions of wikipedia on hackernews and ranked them, and turn them into a scrollable feed to make it a bit more easy to digest &#x2F; interact with. You can see where people submitted the articles and where people mentioned it in comments.<p>The top shows the most frequently linked articles.<p>Random is random but slightly weighted towards the more frequently linked articles (a little bit like Wikipedia actually).<p>Rising is ranked towards the past 12 months.<p>The entire site is served only through the frontend -- I&#x27;ll add more features if people enjoy this!
