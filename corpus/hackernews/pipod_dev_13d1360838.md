# Show HN: Pi pod – Run your pi coding agent in sandboxes on your own server

> Source: hackernews | Category: news | 2026-10-04T15:31:39Z

Score: 108 | Comments: 41

pi pod runs sessions of the pi coding agent in isolated sandboxes (&quot;pods&quot;) on a server you run, in composable environments.<p>----<p>Since moving my company towards AI-native work, I have been really frustrated by the state of &quot;agentic engineering&quot; environments. Products by the labs (claude code, codex) lock you into a single provider for your tokens. Agnostic solutions (factory, devin, arguably cursor) make you pay per-token costs. None of these products allow you to fully customize the harness, and of course they all run on someone else&#x27;s infrastructure.<p>I&#x27;ve been an early and fervent user of pi, which I think is fantastically simple and beautiful software. I have felt it needs an environment for it to work across platforms with fully functional composability for teams.<p>This is very much a work in progress, but for my team this has been a much needed solution and has helped us tremendously. I hope you will give it a try and let me know how you would like it to improve.
