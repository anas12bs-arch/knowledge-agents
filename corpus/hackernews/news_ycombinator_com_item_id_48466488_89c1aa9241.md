# Ask HN: Favorite text heavy blogs the are a joy to read

> Source: hackernews | Category: news | 2026-06-09T23:05:50Z

Score: 21 | Comments: 5

After ten years I’m working on a redesign of my personal tech blog. I want to look at some other blogs with good modern design. Of course searching for ‘well designed blogs’ or ‘webdesign 2026’ brings up endless marketing listicles. Even after wading through the crap, the advice is focused on advertising and product centric sites, not prose heavy sites with good typography.<p>So my question to you is: <i>What are some of your favorite text heavy blogs that are a joy to read?</i><p>I don’t mean the content (though that helps too), but rather sites where the actual experience of reading is pleasant. Think: good fonts, sticky headers (or not), well formatted code snippets, and responsive images.  Slide in navbars vs inline table of contents.<p>Thanks,
