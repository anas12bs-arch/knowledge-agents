# Show HN: An icon for every city in North America

> Source: hackernews | Category: news | 2026-10-22T23:32:08Z

Score: 15 | Comments: 7

To my knowledge there is no icon pack for cities in NA below a certain size.  So I created one!  An icon for every city in NA with &gt;100k population.  544 in total.  Generated with Nano Banana Pro.  These cost me ~$70 to generate.  Giving away for free!  Some exceptions are 1 city for each US state that didn&#x27;t have a city above 100k population such as Burlington Vermont.  I also took liberties with what icon was used such as Whale Tales for BTV.  Surprisingly, smaller cities have many competing landmarks and it can be hard to choose! Feel free to contribute corrections.
