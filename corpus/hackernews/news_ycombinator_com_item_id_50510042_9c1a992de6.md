# Ask HN: Stripe closed us after one $1,500 invoice and never asked for evidence

> Source: hackernews | Category: news | 2026-11-16T01:05:25Z

Score: 149 | Comments: 53

We&#x27;re a small business that sells pre-booked appointments with homeowners to local contractors, billed per appointment that actually happens.<p>Our first client agreed to this on a recorded call and saved his card through a $1 Stripe payment link, which we planned to use for billing later. That $1 payment went through.<p>A few weeks later we invoiced him $1,500 for 3 completed appointments and charged the saved card. Stripe blocked the payment. Two days after that the account was closed with this wording: payments &quot;don&#x27;t appear to have been authorized by the customer.&quot;<p>What happened next:<p>- We appealed through the Dashboard with an appeal letter, our company documents and the invoice. It was rejected the next day as an &quot;unacceptable level of risk.&quot;<p>- Support told us our &quot;business model presents a level of risk we&#x27;re unable to support&quot; and that the decision is final.<p>- After a Stripe employee escalated it internally, we got a short email saying Stripe still won&#x27;t accept payments for our business.<p>In all of this, nobody asked us for the call recording, our messages with the client, or a confirmation from the client himself. As far as we know, he never disputed anything, and he has told us he is paying by bank transfer outside Stripe.<p>I&#x27;m not saying our setup is perfect. It&#x27;s a new account, the first real charge was large compared to the $1 test, and billing a saved card later is probably what looked wrong. If there&#x27;s something specific we should fix, I&#x27;ll fix it.<p>What I&#x27;m asking:<p>1. Is there anyone at Stripe who can look at this with the evidence, including getting the client on a call?<p>2. For people who&#x27;ve been through this: how would you structure per-appointment billing so it doesn&#x27;t look unauthorized (an invoice with a link each time, an email confirmation before each charge, something else)?<p>I can share the account details, the call recording and the messages with anyone from Stripe who wants to look. Thanks for reading.
