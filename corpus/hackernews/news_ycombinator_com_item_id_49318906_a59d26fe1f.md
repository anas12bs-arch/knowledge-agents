# Ask HN: Do you know of any company that went back to hand-written code?

> Source: hackernews | Category: news | 2026-08-16T11:54:39Z

Score: 12 | Comments: 3

This is intended as a question about the current phase of the LLM hype cycle, and, at the same time, as a reality check about whether reluctance to rely on LLMs is economically viable for an average developer.<p>You&#x27;ve definitely seen that many companies progressed from &quot;use LLMs for anything and everything&quot; policies to limiting budgets and scopes of approved LLM usage. It wouldn&#x27;t be unexpected to see that some companies might decide that generating more code faster doesn&#x27;t meaningfully contribute to their bottom line, and forego LLMs in favour of human authoring, but does it ever happen?<p>I&#x27;m not trying to point out any (de)merits of the new way of software development, I simply want to know if there are many&#x2F;any companies that went back to the old ways.
