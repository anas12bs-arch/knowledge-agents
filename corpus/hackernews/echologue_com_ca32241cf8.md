# Show HN: Echologue – the private AI voice journal I built for myself

> Source: hackernews | Category: news | 2026-07-29T13:27:36Z

Score: 3 | Comments: 3

I have tried journaling many times but nothing stuck. So I decided to make my own app for myself with these features:<p>* Voice first
* Private first
* AI chat
* Automatic tagging, meaning extraction, and semantic retrieval using embeddings stored locally
* Export to LLM<p>the AI angle is especially interesting for me. It allows me to ask questions like:<p>* &quot;remind me highlights and crazy nights in the last 3 months&quot;
* &quot;How was I feeling during my trip in Spain and how big of a problem was my breakup&quot;
* &quot;How would you judge my overall interactions with Marc after I told him about my problems&quot;<p>the overall process is fire and forget. I just talk for let&#x27;s say a minute every day or when there was something of interest.<p>Everything is stored on device. The AI inference is done with Zero Data Retention Endpoints. No email or anything else is kept, you are completely anonymous, I have no idea what or how people use this.<p>Otherwise journaling doesn&#x27;t work. I can&#x27;t say my secret thoughts when I know someone has a master key or can suffer a security breach.<p>I hope you enjoy the product as much as I do.
