# Show HN: Loft Day – a wedding invitation turned point-and-click game

> Source: hackernews | Category: news | 2026-08-19T01:37:28Z

Score: 6 | Comments: 2

I wanted to make a fun, wholesome experience for our wedding website. Before long, it had turned into Loft Day: a point-and-click browser game with ten rooms, a party, a road trip, and a lot of smaller games, apps, and hidden interactions.<p>Most of it lives in one 8.8 MB HTML file—roughly 138,000 lines of HTML, SVG, CSS, and JavaScript, with no framework or build step. It also runs a few real runtimes inside the game, including Python through Pyodide, a small Linux system through v86, and, of course, classic shooter games.<p>I developed it over about a month and 5,700 commits, working with Claude and Codex while directing, testing, and repeatedly changing my mind.<p>The source is here: <a href="https:&#x2F;&#x2F;github.com&#x2F;behdad&#x2F;marketa.behdad.org" rel="nofollow">https:&#x2F;&#x2F;github.com&#x2F;behdad&#x2F;marketa.behdad.org</a><p>I’d love to hear technical questions, browser reports, and optimization tips for a game of this scale and stack. But first and foremost: wander.
