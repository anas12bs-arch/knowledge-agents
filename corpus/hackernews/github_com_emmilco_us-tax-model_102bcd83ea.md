# Show HN: A proof-of-concept formalization of the IRS federal tax forms

> Source: hackernews | Category: news | 2026-10-29T20:30:41Z

Score: 23 | Comments: 3

The linked repo is 100% vibe coded, but it was built on a handful of principles:<p>- Most tax software derives trust from the party who maintains it (e.g. Intuit, the IRS, etc.)<p>- Vibe coded software lacks this trustworthiness.<p>- Therefore any formalization should build in verifiability and mechanisms that make it possible for users&#x2F;maintainers to trace claims&#x2F;interpretations back to authoritative source documents.<p>It&#x27;s very much a back-burner passive project right now (do not use this for anything real!), but I feel strongly that something like this ought to exist for the public good. I built it to get a sense of what the shape of what such a thing might be.
