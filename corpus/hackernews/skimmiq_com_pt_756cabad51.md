# Show HN: A puzzle I couldn't solve for a year – cubers cracked it in an hour

> Source: hackernews | Category: news | 2026-08-24T20:00:59Z

Score: 5 | Comments: 0

Dev here. I created a puzzle that looks like Rubik&#x27;s cube but works differently. Instead of turning faces, closed bands of 12 stickers slide around the cube. Every scramble is solvable. I built on-device AI solvers that solve it in about 60 moves (sometimes substantially less than that)<p>I&#x27;m not even a cuber so since I created it I&#x27;ve been thinking that no human can solve it. Until this Tuesday when I showed it to r&#x2F;Cubers. It took them about an hour to find a solution. The first human solve was about 240 moves using mostly (how they call it) 4-move commutators: <a href="https:&#x2F;&#x2F;www.reddit.com&#x2F;r&#x2F;Cubers&#x2F;comments&#x2F;1vrq8zk&#x2F;" rel="nofollow">https:&#x2F;&#x2F;www.reddit.com&#x2F;r&#x2F;Cubers&#x2F;comments&#x2F;1vrq8zk&#x2F;</a><p>So my belief died, but a better question was born: nobody has a teachable method yet, let alone approaching 60 moves.<p>The link is a 60-second browser demo.
