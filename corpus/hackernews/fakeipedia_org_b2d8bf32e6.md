# Show HN: Fakeipedia – Wikipedia for worlds that don't exist

> Source: hackernews | Category: news | 2026-10-17T07:02:35Z

Score: 24 | Comments: 9

Hi HN!<p>Fakeipedia is an automatic world-building platform which takes an imagined world and makes a whole wiki that you can endlessly explore by clicking red links.<p>I came up with this idea a few months ago after playing around with worldbuilding in Gemini, but I didn&#x27;t want to release it until a powerful enough model could do it justice, and I decided that Argon is creative enough to write articles that are worth reading. I was surprised how well it turned out and how engaging some of the writing actually is.<p>If you want to generate an article there is a nominal charge attached ($0.90), which helps cover AI&#x2F;Stripe costs, and I think I just about break even. You can also steer the generation ala choose-your-own-adventure books when generating a new article. Everything generated is free and public for everyone to see.<p>I hope you like it and I&#x27;m very much open to hearing about new world ideas&#x2F;features that would make this project better.
