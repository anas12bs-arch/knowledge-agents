# Show HN: PharmA2A, an open protocol for pharmacy-to-supplier orders on A2A

> Source: hackernews | Category: news | 2026-11-13T07:21:04Z

Score: 5 | Comments: 0

I&#x27;m a pharmacist in France and a nerd. I built an open source protocol based on A2A, Called PharmA2A. Why, because normally I order directly from more than hundred of suppliers, by phone mails or on the web forms. 
The idea is to automatise orders of selling and buying between pharmacies and their suppliers. 
Since 12 November, PharmA2A is live on GH. 
Is anyone of you is using A2A in real life ?
