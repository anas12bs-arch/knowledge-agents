Alot of companies (Including the one i work at fulltime) use third party email builders to provide custom email functionality to users. However this is a niche sector and the prices are... insane?<p>So to try and fix this i built Maillune, a embeddable emailbuilder sdk that is alot cheaper with a usable free tier for hobby projects. The entire goal was to make the features transparent and logical. With lower focus on feature paywalls and aiming on a more &quot;Out of the box&quot; approach.<p>This is one of my recent projects ive built in parallel with my fulltime job.
