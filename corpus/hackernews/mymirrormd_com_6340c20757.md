# Show HN: MirrorMD – An AI second opinion from a facial cosmetic surgeon

> Source: hackernews | Category: news | 2026-10-17T19:27:19Z

Score: 5 | Comments: 2

Hi HN, I am a facial cosmetic surgeon and have been practicing for just over 20 years.  I work in Beverly Hills and Miami and my practice is both surgical and non-surgical (ie fillers) and I specialize in very natural results.<p>I always tell patients: the procedures are easy to do but making it look natural is the hard part.  I am constantly surprised at the interactions I have with patients who don&#x27;t have the experience to know when another provider is selling them on treatments that I know either don&#x27;t work or can even cause harm long-term.  I also realize I can only serve 1 patient at a time.<p>I wanted to try democratize the consultation experience and give patients the information needed to make an informed decision based upon the published literature and my own clinical experience.<p>The website I created is called MyMirrorMD.com and is designed to help patients know what treatments would be supported for <i>their</i> unique face, in what volumes&#x2F;amount and approximate price points.  I&#x27;ve also added a section called &quot;guide&quot; that compares common treatments and answers many of the questions I have fielded day after day for many years.<p>Would love to know your feedback on this project, which is my first foray into using AI and building a website!
