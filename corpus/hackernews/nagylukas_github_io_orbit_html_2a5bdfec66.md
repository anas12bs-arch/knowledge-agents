# Show HN: Orbit – AR satellite tracker, watch 15k+ objects

> Source: hackernews | Category: news | 2026-07-11T19:19:21Z

Score: 10 | Comments: 2

Hey HN! I made Orbit, an iOS app that allows you to see the satellites, planets, and constellations above you in AR, on a 2D map, and on a 3D globe.
The app includes more than 15,000 objects tracked by CelesTrack, together with their pass predictions, descriptions, and detailed orbital data.
A searchable catalog of all objects is also available, as well as a built-in chatbot designed to answer any space-related questions you might have.<p>This is my first published iOS app, so any feedback is greatly appreciated!
