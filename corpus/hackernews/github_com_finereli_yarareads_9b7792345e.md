# Show HN: Open-source early-reading reader with phonics help

> Source: hackernews | Category: news | 2026-10-19T19:29:30Z

Score: 11 | Comments: 1

My kid is 6 y&#x2F;o and she is learning to read. She&#x27;s moved through the basic phonics with Teach Your Monster to Read but was struggling with how repetitive it was. I wanted to give her some stories to read, but she still needed help sounding words out - and I wanted her to have the experience of sitting and reading by herself.<p>So I built this book reader app to allow her to click on the words, sound out the phonemes, and read by herself.<p>My 12 year old son wrote the first story, there&#x27;s a cliffhanger at the end and he&#x27;s working on the next section.<p>We&#x27;re going to release more chapters and more stories in the near future.<p>And there&#x27;s one other thing -<p>The reader has an MCP connection, so you can connect your Claude or ChatGPT or Grok, give them a PDF of a book you like (or even a set of scans done quickly with a phone camera) and have them import your favorite books into the app (private to your account, for your own kid, if you own the book of course).<p>The reader is free to use on its website and open source if you want to tinker and create your own version.<p>The included stories are under Creative Commons.
