Score: 4 | Comments: 0

For ideation, for market testing, for go to market, for marketing content generation?<p>Why have a startup incubator full of Stanford grads when you can just replace them with a data center and keep all the profit?<p>More importantly, if that is the general temperature of the business space, it seems like acquiring resources is more important than innovating?
