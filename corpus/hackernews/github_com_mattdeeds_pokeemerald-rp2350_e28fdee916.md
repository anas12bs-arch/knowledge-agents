# Show HN: Pokémon Emerald Ported to Raspberry Pi Pico 2

> Source: hackernews | Category: news | 2026-08-07T00:28:11Z

Score: 36 | Comments: 16

Pokémon Emerald ported to the RP2350 microcontroller. No emulator, 60 fps HDMI output. Recompiled from ARMv4T to Cortex-M33 and the Game Boy Advance&#x27;s video hardware is reimplemented in software on the second core.
