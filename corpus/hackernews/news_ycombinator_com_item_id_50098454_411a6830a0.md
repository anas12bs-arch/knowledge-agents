# Show HN: My Terminal Themes Web App

> Source: hackernews | Category: news | 2026-10-15T05:11:04Z

Score: 8 | Comments: 3

Well, after 15 years of saying &quot;Someday I&#x27;ll turn this into a web app,&quot; I finally did it!<p>I&#x27;ve been maintaining a popular repo of terminal themes at <a href="https:&#x2F;&#x2F;github.com&#x2F;mbadolato&#x2F;iTerm2-Color-Schemes" rel="nofollow">https:&#x2F;&#x2F;github.com&#x2F;mbadolato&#x2F;iTerm2-Color-Schemes</a>, and it was finally time to build the app with all the features I wanted!<p>So, the next-gen version is live at <a href="https:&#x2F;&#x2F;terminalthemes.com" rel="nofollow">https:&#x2F;&#x2F;terminalthemes.com</a>! It currently has over 800 themes, and provides downloads for close to 40 different formats (iTerm, Ghostty, Windows, and many more)<p>It&#x27;s free for everyone. An account is not needed to browse and download, but registering for an account (free) allows you to vote, bookmark, submit themes, etc.<p>I focused on a11y features for those who need it, in addition to adding Find My Theme and other tools to make it easy to find something you like!<p>Live terminal and code previews help show a more realistic view of the terminal, as opposed to the ANSI screenshots used at the GitHub repository. Those are still there, for those who like them.<p>I would love for you guys to check it out and provide feedback, feature suggestions, whatever!<p>And a huge thanks to the community of people that have contributed themes, ports to other terminals, and tools to provide conversions between formats over the years.<p>Oh! And if you&#x27;ve ever contributed themes to the GitHub repo, please register for an account, find your theme(s), and hit the Claim Authorship button!
