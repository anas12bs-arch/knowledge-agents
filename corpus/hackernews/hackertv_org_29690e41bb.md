# Show HN: HackerTV – HN's most-recommended Youtube videos as 24/7 TV channels

> Source: hackernews | Category: news | 2026-11-04T02:24:32Z

Score: 184 | Comments: 38

I saw HackerTube (<a href="https:&#x2F;&#x2F;news.ycombinator.com&#x2F;item?id=50319510">https:&#x2F;&#x2F;news.ycombinator.com&#x2F;item?id=50319510</a>) a few days ago, the YouTube videos HN keeps
recommending, pulled from 19 years of comments. It made me want to flip
through them instead of picking, so I built a TV for it. I have Algo fatigue.<p>Each topic area is a channel (DEVTV, SOLDER, RETRO, ORBIT, ...), plus HN Prime
(the top 400), a music channel, and Late Night on ch 99, which only broadcasts
11pm-5am your local time and shows color bars otherwise.<p>Similar to HackerTube it was built with Claude Code in a few hours.<p>All credit for the data goes to HackerTube. Happy to hear channel ideas.
