# Show HN: I built a free app for New Yorkers to save money on groceries

> Source: hackernews | Category: news | 2026-07-10T01:49:18Z

Score: 11 | Comments: 6

I built this because I see that grocery savings are achievable in NYC. People usually just go to the store they&#x27;re used to going to, and it&#x27;s rarely worth the effort of combing through card cashback, weekly coupons, CPG rebates.<p>Most people leave real money on the table by not stacking them, and even more don&#x27;t even know that these deals are out there.... so I built a way to automate it.<p>You can use it for free, no login, currently NYC-only with ~690 stores.<p>I built it so that you just search whatever you want (use commas if you want to search multiple items). Or - use the AI tool to help shop for you. If you&#x27;re curious, it&#x27;s powered by a trained LLama model.<p>Honest limitations are coverage and freshness. Id love some feedback on where the data looks wrong or is stale.<p>Question for the room - what to prioritize if you&#x27;re working with messy, multi-source retail&#x2F;pricing data? Is freshness or coverage the top priority if you cant get a uniform response from every source? curious on what to prioritize here.
